# 059 — Gemini API Pricing and Quotas: Complete 2026 Guide
- **Source**: https://www.aifreeapi.com/en/posts/gemini-api-pricing-and-quotas (AIFreeAPI, 2026-01)
- **Claim**: A practical reference to Gemini API token pricing and quota mechanics as of January 2026, including the December 2025 free-tier quota cuts, with cost-estimation examples and optimization strategies (batch, caching, model selection).
- **Mechanism**: Pricing is token-based (input/output priced separately per 1M tokens), with a 2x long-context multiplier above 200K tokens on most models. Quotas enforced on four dimensions: RPM (requests/min, rolling 60s), TPM (tokens/min), RPD (requests/day, resets midnight PT), and IPM (images/min), governed by a token-bucket algorithm. Tiers: Free (no card), Tier 1 (enable Cloud Billing, instant), Tier 2 (request upgrade), Enterprise (negotiated). Optimization: Batch API (flat 50% discount, 24h SLA), context caching (store repeated system prompts/docs server-side), prompt trimming, tiered model escalation (Flash-Lite→Flash→Pro), token-efficiency techniques.
- **Numbers**: Per 1M tokens — Gemini 3 Pro Preview $2.00 in / $12.00 out (≤200K; $4.00/$18.00 above); Gemini 2.5 Pro $1.25/$10.00 (≤200K), $2.50/$20.00 (>200K); Gemini 2.5 Flash $0.30 in / $2.50 out (text/image/video; audio $0.70 in); Flash-Lite $0.10 in / $0.40 out. Free-tier quotas: 5 RPM (2.5 Pro) to 15 RPM (Flash-Lite), 250,000 TPM universal, up to 1,000 RPD. Dec 2025 RPD cuts: 2.5 Pro ~250→100 (−60%); 2.5 Flash ~250→20–50 (−80 to −92%); Flash-Lite ~500→1000 (+100%). Context caching "up to 75% savings"; batch 50% savings; Tier 1 context caching cited as "75% savings on repeated prompts." Image gen (Nano Banana Pro): input ~$0.0011/image (560 tokens), output ~$0.039/image at 1024x1024 (1290 tokens @ $30/M) up to ~$0.24/image at 4096x4096. Grounding with Google Search: first 1,500 queries/day free on paid tiers, then $35 per 1,000 queries. Token heuristics: ~4 chars/token, 1,000 words≈1,333 tokens; image=560 tokens; video 258 tokens/sec at 1 FPS; audio 25 tokens/sec. Cost examples: light Flash-Lite $0.60/mo; startup 2.5 Flash $46.50/mo (→~$40 with caching); heavy mixed Pro+Flash $592/mo (→~$450 with batch). 2.5 Pro auto-switches to Flash after ~10–15 prompts on free tier.
- **Token-cost implication**: Match model to task (Flash-Lite for chat/classification/real-time, Flash for most production, Pro/3 Pro only for complex reasoning/code); use context caching for repeated system prompts/docs and the Batch API for non-urgent work to cut 50–75%; keep single long-context requests under the 200K threshold to avoid the 2x multiplier; enable Cloud Billing (Tier 1) for production since the Dec 2025 free tier is now a testing-only ground. Free-tier prompts may be used to train Google models — upgrade to a paid tier if privacy matters.
- **Caveats**: Third-party blog (AIFreeAPI/laozhang.ai), not official Google docs; "before" Dec-2025 RPD figures are approximate (~250, ~500) and change percentages derived from them. Preview pricing (3 Pro) is provisional with a temporary free-tier waiver and expected to settle (~$1.50/$10). Enterprise discount figures (30–40%, $25k/mo threshold) are described generally.
