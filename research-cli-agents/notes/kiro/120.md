# 120 — Cursor account pricing (Models & Pricing)
- **Source**: https://cursor.com/docs/account/pricing (Cursor, undated)
- **Claim**: Cursor is sold inside "The X Subscription" with a single weekly pool of usage for Cursor's own models shared across Grok and Cursor; third-party models (Claude/GPT/Gemini) are billed at API list price from prepaid credit on Premium and above.
- **Mechanism**: Each paid plan includes one weekly-refreshing pool for Cursor models (Grok 4.7/4.6/4.5, Composer 2.5), shared across editor Agent, CLI, Cloud Agents, Grok Bot, and Grok apps; different models draw from the pool at different rates. Third-party models aren't in the pool — on Premium+ they bill at API list price from prepaid credit from the first request (Plus/Free have no third-party models). Top-ups add prepaid credit (manual or Auto Top-up below a threshold); a monthly additional-usage limit caps paid overage. Auto has three modes: Cost (classic, bills as Cursor-model from the weekly pool), Balance, Intelligence (Teams/Enterprise Cursor Router routes to Cursor or third-party model at that model's list price). Regional data residency adds a 10% uplift. Max Mode (legacy request plans only) extends context at API rate +20%.
- **Numbers**: Plans: Free $0; Plus $8/mo (base); Premium $30/mo (4x Plus); Super $100/mo (15x); Ultra $200/mo (50x); Teams $30/$100/$200 per seat; Enterprise custom. Annual: Plus $80/yr, Premium $300/yr, Super $1,000/yr, Ultra $2,000/yr. Cursor Token Rate (Teams/Enterprise) $0.25 per 1M tokens on eligible third-party requests (input+output+cached); exempt if cache-read price strictly < $0.10/1M across every tier, and exempt for Cursor models and Cost mode. Regional residency +10%; Max Mode +20%.
- **Token-cost implication**: On individual plans keep routine work on pooled Cursor models (Composer 2.5 is cheapest at $0.5/$2.5 in/out) and reserve pricey third-party models (Opus/Fable/Sol) for hard tasks since they draw prepaid credit from the first request; exploit low cache-read prices (Haiku $0.01, GPT Luna $0.02); on Teams/Enterprise factor the extra $0.25/1M Cursor Token Rate unless the model's cache-read is <$0.10; avoid regional residency (+10%) and legacy Max Mode (+20%) unless needed.
- **Caveats**: Official vendor pricing page, undated; prices are list rates and model lineup is a specific snapshot (Grok 4.x, Claude 5.5, GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.x/4). Weekly pool "multipliers" (4x/15x/50x) are relative to an unspecified Plus "base" amount — no absolute token quota given. Cursor Token Rate and residency/Max Mode surcharges are conditional.
