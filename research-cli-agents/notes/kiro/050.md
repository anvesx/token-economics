# 050 — TinyLLM: Evaluation and Optimization of Small Language Models for Agentic Tasks on Edge Devices
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22138 (arXiv, 2025-11)
- **Claim**: Evaluates small language models (<3B params) for agentic function/tool/API calling on edge devices using the Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard (BFCL); medium SLMs (1–3B) markedly outperform ultra-compact (<1B) models, and hybrid optimization (SFT + preference/RL) is needed for accurate, stable on-device agents.
- **Mechanism**: Benchmarks TinyAgent, TinyLlama, Qwen, xLAM across BFCL categories (simple, multiple, parallel, parallel-multiple, relevance detection) in live, non-live (AST-level), and multi-turn settings. Optimization strategies: supervised fine-tuning (SFT full/partial), parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), reinforcement learning (PPO/actor-critic), Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and hybrid SFT→RL pipelines. A DPO pipeline is built from AgentBank data (ALFRED): the AgentBank response is the "chosen" entity and TinyLlama-generated responses are "rejected," with manual validation. Dataset = Gorilla OpenFunctions expanded 100→2,000 points across 40 sub-domains, ~60% AST-based evaluation.
- **Numbers**: Overall BFCL accuracy — xLAM-2-3b-fc-r (FC) 65.74% overall (81.03% live, 88.22% non-live AST, 55.62% multi-turn); Qwen3-4B (Prompt) 62.04% overall (75.52% live, 82.58% non-live, 35.25% multi-turn); Qwen3-1.7B 55.49% (multi-turn 16.88%); xLAM-2-1b-fc-r 53.97% (multi-turn 8.38%, but 35.25% in the multi-turn table); Qwen3-0.6B 45.76% (multi-turn 1.38%); TinyLlama-1.1B-32k-Instruct and TinyAgent-1.1B ~19.7% overall, 20% non-live, 0% multi-turn. Ultra-compact models only succeed at irrelevance/relevance detection (abstention). Multi-turn best: xLAM-2-3b 55.62% overall (base 69.50%, long-ctx 41.00%). Model preference: <3B params, ideally medium 1–3B.
- **Token-cost implication**: For edge/on-device agents, a well-optimized 1–3B model (xLAM-2-3B, Qwen3-4B) can deliver usable tool-calling without cloud, cutting inference latency, connectivity dependence, privacy risk, and GPU/cloud cost; ultra-compact (<1.1B) models are unsuitable for agentic tasks beyond abstention. Use a layered optimization budget: PEFT-based SFT for efficiency, DPO for stable alignment (cheaper/more sample-efficient than PPO), and hybrid RL only where adaptability is worth the extra compute.
- **Caveats**: Accuracy numbers are self-reported on BFCL; some table inconsistencies (xLAM-2-1b multi-turn reported as both 8.38% and 35.25%). DPO pipeline partial — only ALFRED converted, ALFWorld in progress, large-scale DPO training stated as future work, so the optimization-method claims largely describe strategies/prior work rather than fully-executed results in this paper. Edge latency/deployment trade-offs discussed qualitatively, not quantified with on-device latency numbers. Academic (Clark Atlanta University et al.).
