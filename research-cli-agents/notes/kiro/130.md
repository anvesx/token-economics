# 130 — The Complexity Trap: Simple Observation Masking Is as Efficient as LLM Summarization for Agent Context Management
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21433 (JetBrains Research, 2025-08)
- **Claim**: A simple Observation Masking strategy (replace old tool outputs with a placeholder) halves agent cost vs an unmanaged Raw Agent while matching or slightly exceeding the solve rate of LLM-based summarization — so the added complexity of LLM-Summary buys little. A hybrid of the two cuts cost further.
- **Mechanism**: In a ReAct/CodeAct trajectory of (reasoning, action, observation) turns, environment observations dominate tokens (~84% of an average SWE-agent turn). Observation Masking (`fmask`) keeps full reasoning/actions but replaces observations older than a rolling window of M turns with a placeholder ("Previous N lines omitted"). LLM-Summary uses a summarizer LLM to condense turns: accumulate N turns, keep last M unaltered, fold older turns into a running summary (bounded context). Hybrid: use Observation Masking during turn accumulation and defer LLM-Summary as a last resort for very long trajectories (N=43, M=W=10). Evaluated in SWE-agent (and OpenHands) on SWE-bench Verified; Observation Masking M=10, LLM-Summary N=21/M=10.
- **Numbers**: Observation tokens ≈84% of an average SWE-agent turn. Turn limit 250. Both strategies cut cost per instance >50% in most configs. Qwen3-Coder 480B: Observation Masking solve 54.8% vs LLM-Summary 53.8% vs Raw 53.4%; 52% cheaper than Raw and $0.03/instance cheaper than LLM-Summary ($15 across 500 instances). Gemini 2.5 Flash: Raw $0.41 → Masking $0.18 (-56.1%), LLM-Summary $0.24 (-41.5%). Hybrid on Verified-50 w/ Qwen3-Coder 480B: -7% vs Masking, -11% vs LLM-Summary, +2.6 pp solve rate; projected savings $20 vs Masking and $35 vs LLM-Summary on full Verified. Summary-generation API cost = up to 7.2% of total instance cost. LLM-Summary trajectory elongation: Gemini mean 52 turns (+15% vs Masking 44, +4% vs Raw 50). Gemini cache hits up to 10× cheaper than misses. Models: Qwen3-32B (122K ctx, YaRN), Qwen3-Coder-480B (256K), Gemini 2.5 Flash (1M).
- **Token-cost implication**: Any context management beats none (unmanaged cost can more than double). Prefer cheap Observation Masking over LLM summarization for SE agents — equal/better quality, lower cost, fewer warm-up turns (M=10 vs N+M=31), robust on short trajectories. LLM summaries incur direct API cost plus a "trajectory elongation" side-effect (summaries act as a reinforcing signal that keeps the agent going). Tune the rolling window M per scaffold. Consider the hybrid to get gains on both short and long trajectories.
- **Caveats**: SE domain only (SWE-bench), where verbose tool outputs naturally favor masking — may not generalize to succinct-interaction domains. All triggers are fixed/non-adaptive heuristics. OpenHands generality shown only on a 50-instance slice; Observation Masking window needs re-tuning per scaffold (reusing SWE-agent's optimum degraded OpenHands badly). Qwen3-32B cost may be inflated (Alibaba pricing doesn't separate cache hit/miss). Critic-enhanced summaries gave no quality gain and worsened elongation. NeurIPS 2025 workshop paper; authors self-report; code/data released.
- **Topic**: compression
