# 061 — 5 Open-Source Tools to Control Your AI API Costs at the Code Level
- **Source**: https://www.finout.io/blog/5-open-source-tools-to-control-your-ai-api-costs-at-the-code-level (Finout, 2026-07-02 on page; metadata 2026-04)
- **Claim**: Five open-source tools cover distinct cost levers: LiteLLM (budget gateway), Langfuse (cost observability), LLMLingua (prompt compression), RouteLLM (model routing), GPTCache (semantic caching).
- **Mechanism**: LiteLLM proxy tracks spend per key/user/team/project, hard max_budget with budget_duration, tag attribution, fallbacks. Langfuse computes cost at ingestion from a pricing DB, tracks reasoning/cached/audio/image tokens, @observe traces. LLMLingua: budget controller, sentence-level perplexity pruning (GPT-2-small), token-level compression; LLMLingua-2 uses distilled BERT encoder. RouteLLM: trained routers (matrix factorization default, similarity, BERT, causal LLM) pick strong or weak model by threshold. GPTCache: embeddings plus vector search, pluggable stores.
- **Numbers**: LLMLingua up to 20x compression with 1.5% GSM8K degradation; LongLLMLingua +21.4% RAG performance at 1/4 tokens; LLMLingua-2 3-6x faster; RAG 4,000-8,000 tokens compressed to 800-2,000 (60-80% cost reduction claim). RouteLLM: MT Bench 85% cost reduction at 95% of GPT-4 quality; MMLU 45%; GSM8K 35%. GPTCache claims up to 10x cost reduction, 2-100x latency on hits. Combined 30-70% (Finout claim). Output tokens cost 3-10x input. $0.05 to $168 per 1M tokens range; 10,000 tickets ~$10 on GPT-4o Mini vs $1,300+ on GPT-5.2 Pro. Gen AI enterprise spend $37B in 2025; per-token costs down ~1,000x over three years; 37% of enterprises spend over $250,000/yr. Google batch 50% off.
- **Token-cost implication**: Layer controls: compress before the call, route and cap during, attribute after. Enforce budgets at a gateway so runaway agents hit a wall.
- **Caveats**: Vendor (Finout) marketing post promoting its own platform; figures are the tools' own published or claimed results, mostly on chat/RAG benchmarks, not coding agents; RouteLLM is binary only; GPTCache is in maintenance mode.
