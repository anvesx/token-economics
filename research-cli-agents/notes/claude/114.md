# 114 — Codex pricing: current costs, usage limits, and realistic budgets (2026)
- **Source**: https://www.jetadmin.io/blog/codex-pricing-current-costs-usage-limits-and-realistic-budgets-2026 (Jetadmin, 2026, facts "as of July 2026")
- **Claim**: Codex has no standalone consumer plan; it is bundled into ChatGPT tiers with rolling 5-hour windows plus token-based credits, and heavy professional use lands at $100-200 per developer per month.
- **Mechanism**: Until 2026-04-02 Codex metered per message; since then credits map to API per-million-token prices (input, cached input, output). Limits are a rolling 5-hour window per account, pooled at workspace level for Business/Enterprise. Image generation drains limits 3-5x faster than text. Fast mode burns credits at a higher rate. Credit costs: reduce via concise prompts, scoped context, caching, cheaper models for routine work, plan-before-implement, diff edits, per-project tagging, fast mode off by default.
- **Numbers**: Free $0, Go $8, Plus $20, Pro 5x $100, Pro 20x $200 (about 5x/20x Plus usage), Business $25/user monthly or $20 annual (min 2 seats). GPT-5.3-Codex API: $1.75 per M input, $14 per M output (8x), cached input 10x cheaper than fresh. 1 credit ~ $0.04. Typical tasks: small fix low thousands of tokens; single-file refactor tens of thousands; repo-wide hundreds of thousands+. Personas: hobbyist $0-8; Plus professional $20-60; Pro power user $100-200+; 10-person team seats $200-250/month plus credits, $100-200 per active developer (attributed to OpenAI guidance). Legacy Codex-only seats closed to new Business workspaces 2026-06-24. Suggested pilot: 2-4 weeks.
- **Token-cost implication**: Output tokens dominate cost (8x input), so cap output, use diffs and plans, route routine tasks to a mini model (GPT-5.4-mini cited), keep cached prefixes stable, and avoid bursty long sessions that hit the 5-hour ceiling.
- **Caveats**: Vendor blog selling Jet Admin (app builder), second-hand pricing, not official; comparisons to Claude Code, Cursor and Copilot are vague; hard spend caps "vary by plan"; model names (GPT-5.3-Codex, 5.4-mini, 5.5) are cited without a rate card for each.
