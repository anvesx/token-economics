# 025 — More with Less: An Empirical Study of Turn-Control Strategies for Efficient Coding Agents
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.16786 (ByteDance authors, arXiv / ICSE '26, 2025-10; v2 25 Nov 2025)
- **Claim**: Capping agent turns at the 75th percentile of an unrestricted baseline cuts cost 24%-68% with minimal solve-rate loss. A dynamic "start small, grant one extension if no patch" policy cuts a further 5%-24% versus the matching fixed limit while matching or beating its solve rate.
- **Mechanism**: TRAE Agent (tools: str_replace_editor, bash, task_done) on a 100-task random subset of SWE-bench Verified with Claude 4 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT 4.1. Three modes: unlimited; fixed limit at the 25th/50th/75th percentile of baseline turns with a per-turn reminder ("You have X turns left to complete the task"); dynamic X->Y, where the agent gets limit X, then a one-time extension to Y with a notice that extra turns were granted. Prompt caching was disabled for all runs. Cost is computed from token counts at list prices.
- **Numbers**: Prices per 1M tokens: Sonnet 4 $3 in/$15 out; Opus 4 $15/$75; Gemini 2.5 Pro $1.25/$10 (<=200K prompt), $2.50/$15 (>200K); GPT 4.1 $2/$8. Unlimited baseline: Sonnet 75% solve, 5,704 turns, $585.21 ($7.80 per solved); Gemini 63%, 3,925 turns, $407.66 ($6.47); GPT 4.1 62%, 5,641 turns, $322.02 ($5.19). Baseline turn percentiles (25/50/75): Sonnet 44/52/63.75; Gemini 19/28.5/44.5; GPT 4.1 38.75/49.5/66.25. Fixed 75th: Sonnet 71% (-5.33%), $446.99 (-23.62%); Gemini 65% (+3.17%), $129.32 (-68.28%); GPT 4.1 57% (-8.06%), $198.26 (-38.43%). Fixed 25th: Sonnet 60%, $312.94 (-46.53%); Gemini 26% (-58.73%), $51.17 (-87.45%), 63 empty patches; GPT 4.1 49%, $120.00 (-62.74%). Without reminders at 25th pct Sonnet falls to 24% solve. Dynamic 25->50 vs Fixed 50: Sonnet 64% vs 63%, $324.45 vs $384.23 (-15.56%); Gemini 55% vs 52%, $77.56 (-5.95%); GPT 4.1 57% vs 57%, $123.41 (-24.30%). Dynamic 50->75 vs Fixed 75: Sonnet 70% vs 71%, $387.00 (-13.42%); Gemini 64% vs 65%, $98.05 (-24.18%); GPT 4.1 59% vs 57%, $173.93 (-12.27%). Baseline input tokens ~159M-240M vs output ~0.47M-4.3M. Median turns on SWE-bench Verified leaderboard agents 41-58, some tasks over 175. Plan caching cited from related work: -46.62% serving cost at 96.67% of optimal performance; CodeAgents cited: input tokens -55% to -87%.
- **Token-cost implication**: Set a turn cap near the 75th percentile of your own observed turn counts, always include a remaining-turns reminder, and prefer a two-stage budget (small, then one extension) over a single large cap. Measure percentiles per model, since Gemini 2.5 Pro collapsed at the 25th percentile.
- **Caveats**: Academic, self-run on 100 SWE-bench Verified tasks, single run per config (no repeats, no confidence intervals in main tables). Case-study repeats (n=10) are not statistically significant at p<0.05. Prompt caching disabled, so real savings may differ. Results depend on TRAE Agent and the reminder wording. Only closed models tested; Sonnet 4 not Opus.
