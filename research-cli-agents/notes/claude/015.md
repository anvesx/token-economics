# 015 — Clarifying our pricing
- **Source**: https://cursor.com/blog/june-2025-pricing (Cursor / Anysphere, 2025-06; updated post-July 4)
- **Claim**: Cursor apologizes for unclear communication of its June 2025 move from request-based Pro pricing to a $20 monthly usage credit pool at API prices, and offers refunds for surprise charges.
- **Mechanism**: Pro = unlimited Tab and Auto-mode models, plus $20/month of frontier-model usage billed at API pricing, with a short grace period after the pool is exhausted; optional spend limit to buy more at cost. Auto routes automatically to different frontier models based on capacity. Previously: 500 requests/month, Sonnet counted as two. Rationale: newer models spend more tokens per request on long-horizon tasks, and the hardest requests cost an order of magnitude more than simple ones, so per-request pricing misrepresents cost.
- **Numbers**: $20/month included frontier usage; old limit 500 requests/month (Sonnet = 2); median-usage equivalents of $20: about 225 Sonnet 4 requests, 550 Gemini requests, or 650 GPT 4.1 requests; refund window June 16 to July 4 (2025).
- **Token-cost implication**: Seat tools are moving to token-metered pricing; budget by dollars-of-API-usage, expect hardest requests to cost ~10x simple ones, and use auto-routing/cheaper models for routine work.
- **Caveats**: Vendor self-report; "most users do not run out" is asserted without data; median-based equivalents only.
