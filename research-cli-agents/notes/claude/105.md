# 105 — Reasoning Effort Tuning: Minimal to xhigh for Cost and Speed
- **Source**: https://codex.danielvaughan.com/2026/03/27/reasoning-effort-tuning (Daniel Vaughan, 2026-03-27)
- **Claim**: Codex CLI's model_reasoning_effort setting (minimal to xhigh) strongly drives cost and speed; match effort to task and role, with high effort for planning/orchestration and low for subagent execution.
- **Mechanism**: Set via config.toml (model_reasoning_effort, plan_mode_reasoning_effort), the -e flag, profiles, or /effort mid-session. Reasoning tokens are billed but not shown. Subagents in TOML each carry their own reasoning_effort (planner high, implementer/tester low, reviewer high). Plan mode defaults to high when unset.
- **Numbers**: Approximate reasoning-token multiplier vs medium: minimal ~0.1x, low ~0.3x, medium 1x, high ~3-5x, xhigh ~8-15x. Orchestrator-high/subagent-low pattern can reduce token spend by 50-70% vs everything at medium. GPT-5.1-Codex-Max beats GPT-5.1-Codex on SWE-bench Verified at medium effort with 30% fewer thinking tokens.
- **Token-cost implication**: Keep medium as default, use profiles to scope high/xhigh, run executor subagents at low/minimal, avoid xhigh as default.
- **Caveats**: Multipliers are approximate, model-dependent, not publicly documented, based on community benchmarks. The 50-70% saving is an unsourced estimate. Third-party blog.
