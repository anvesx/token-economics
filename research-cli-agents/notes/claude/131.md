# 131 — Budget-Aware Tool-Use Enables Effective Agent Scaling
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17006 (Google DeepMind / Google Cloud AI Research, 2025-11; v2 dated 17 Aug 2026)
- **Claim**: Raising an agent's tool-call budget alone does not help because agents lack budget awareness and plateau; a lightweight Budget Tracker (and the fuller BATS framework) that shows remaining budget yields better accuracy at lower cost.
- **Mechanism**: Budget Tracker is a prompt plug-in: after each tool response a `<budget>` tag shows remaining units, and the prompt tells the agent to adapt (HIGH >=70% remaining: broad 3-5 searches; MEDIUM 30%-70%: 2-3 precise queries; LOW 10%-30%: 1 focused query; CRITICAL <10%: avoid the depleted tool). BATS also adapts planning and verification, with a verification module enabling early stopping, and drops historical tool responses to shorten context. A unified cost metric sums token cost (input, output, cache-hit priced separately) and tool-call cost.
- **Numbers**: Tool calls priced at $0.001 per invocation. Gemini-2.5-Pro, BrowseComp: Budget Tracker at budget 10 gives 12.8% vs ReAct at budget 100 giving 12.6%, with 40.4% fewer search calls, 19.9% fewer browse calls and 31.3% lower overall cost. Another study: at budget 10 Avg@4 rises from 47.8% to 55.5% and Pass^4 from 21.9% to 27.2%; beats baseline at budget 50 (27.2% vs 26.3%) with about 18.4% fewer tool calls (5.38 vs 6.59). BATS reaches 24.6% on BrowseComp, 46.0% on BrowseComp-ZH, 27.0% on HLE-Search with Gemini-2.5-Pro. Removing verification drops BrowseComp from 18.7% to 15.4%. BATS vs BATS-response (keeps full tool responses): 24.6% vs 24.3% accuracy; cache tokens 39.3 vs 91.8 (units of 10,000). SWE-bench Verified at budget 50: resolve rate 55.4% vs 36.8% with similar calls (45.7 vs 46.0) and token cost (18.88 cents vs 18.50 cents); vs baseline budget 60: 9.9% fewer tool calls (45.7 vs 50.7), 7.5% lower token cost (18.88 vs 20.40 cents), +2.2 points (55.4% vs 53.2%). ReAct plateaus at budget 100 on BrowseComp; only 0.8% of BrowseComp data needs more than 50 calls for Gemini-2.5-Pro. Max new tokens 65,536 (Gemini) and 64,000 (Claude); Gemini-2.5-Pro thinking budget 1024.
- **Token-cost implication**: Telling the agent its remaining budget cuts spend without losing accuracy, and pruning old tool responses cuts input and cache tokens substantially. Cost per solved task falls at equal or higher accuracy.
- **Caveats**: Mostly web-search benchmarks (BrowseComp, BrowseComp-ZH, HLE-Search) with Gemini-2.5 and Claude-Sonnet-4; SWE-bench shown as an extension. Absolute accuracies are low (12-25% on BrowseComp). Tool price is a fixed $0.001 average. Tool-call budget, not a token cap.
