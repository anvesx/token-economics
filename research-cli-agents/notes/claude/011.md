# 011 — Claude Code Limits: 5-Hour Caps Doubled in May 2026
- **Source**: https://www.verdent.ai/guides/claude-code-limits-doubled-may-2026 (Verdent, 2026-05)
- **Claim**: In May 2026 Anthropic doubled Claude Code 5-hour rate limits on all paid plans, removed the peak-hour throttle, raised Opus API rate limits, and temporarily raised weekly limits 50%.
- **Mechanism**: The 5-hour window is a rolling usage limit over the last 300 minutes; the weekly cap is a harder ceiling. May 6: 5-hour limits doubled, peak-hour throttle (said to cut Pro/Max quota by up to half in busy windows) removed. May 13: weekly limits +50% for Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise, expiring July 13, 2026 unless extended. May 15: all 5-hour and weekly counters reset once. Free plan excluded.
- **Numbers**: Pro $20/mo: roughly 45 prompts per window becomes roughly 90. Max 5x $100/mo: weekly Opus 4.7 budget about 50 h to about 75 h. Max 20x $200/mo: about 200 h to about 300 h. Weekly +50% expires 2026-07-13. Claimed: three days of typical usage now costs what one day did in April. Cited capacity: Colossus 1, more than 300 MW and 220,000+ NVIDIA GPUs.
- **Token-cost implication**: Under subscription limits, model-downshifting and peak-hour scheduling matter less; the article suggests trying Opus 4.7 for work previously sent to Sonnet, but plan for the weekly bump expiring rather than depending on it.
- **Caveats**: Third-party vendor blog (Verdent sells a competing Claude-based tool); no primary Anthropic source quoted; prompt counts are approximate and vary with message length and model; the stated cause (SpaceX compute deal, Codex competition) is the author's interpretation.
