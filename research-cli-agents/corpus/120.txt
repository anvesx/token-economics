Models & Pricing

Cursor supports frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, SpaceXSI, and more. Cursor is sold as part of The X Subscription, one subscription for Grok, Cursor, and X. Paid plans include a weekly pool of usage for Cursor models, shared across Grok and Cursor. Third-party models aren't included; on Premium and above you can use them at API list price, paid from prepaid credit.

On Pro, Pro+, Ultra, Start, or a Teams Standard or Premium seat? Those are legacy Cursor plans. They keep working as they are, with two monthly usage pools and on-demand usage.

Plans

Premium$30/mo4x PlusAPI list price, from prepaid credit
Super$100/mo15x PlusAPI list price, from prepaid credit

EnterpriseCustomPooledAPI list price plus the Cursor Token Rate on eligible models

Annual billing is available at $80/yr for Plus, $300/yr for Premium, $1,000/yr for Super, and $2,000/yr for Ultra. Prices exclude tax.

Free. Agent and Chat with limited usage.

Plus. Agent with Cursor's own models (Grok 4.7, Grok 4.6, Grok 4.5, and Composer 2.5) at fixed settings: Fast is off, effort is medium, and Grok 4.7 uses the 256k context window. Also Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine, Build, and Grok API access. No Auto, Cloud Agents, Automations, Bugbot, Origin, third-party models, or your own API keys.

Premium. Everything in Plus, with 4x the usage. Adds Cloud Agents and Automations, code hosting on Origin, code review with Bugbot, third-party models at API list price, and Grok Bot.

Super. Everything in Premium, with 15x Plus usage and priority model access at peak times.

Ultra. Everything in Super, with 50x Plus usage and earliest access to new products and models.

Premium suits new Agent users, Super suits daily Agent users, and Ultra suits power users running several agents or Automations. See cursor.com/pricing for the full feature comparison, including Grok and X benefits.

Included usage

Each paid plan includes one pool of usage for Cursor models. The pool is shared across everything on your subscription: Agent in the editor, CLI, Cloud Agents, Grok Bot, and the Grok apps. It refreshes weekly, so one heavy day doesn't use up your month.

Different models draw from the pool at different rates. Your remaining usage and next reset date are shown in your editor settings and under Usage at grok.com/dashboard.

Cursor models

The pool covers Grok 4.7, Grok 4.6, Grok 4.5, and Composer 2.5. On Teams and Enterprise plans, Cursor Router picks among Cursor models and third-party models for each Balance or Intelligence request based on your optimization mode; a third-party route bills as third-party usage.

Top-ups and spending caps

Top-ups add prepaid credit to your subscription. Credit pays for two kinds of additional usage:

Third-party models in Cursor, from the first request, at API list price.

Cursor models beyond your weekly pool, at the rates in the table above.

Buy credit when you choose, or turn on Auto Top-up to refill when your balance drops below a threshold you set. A monthly additional usage limit caps how much paid additional usage you can consume each month, regardless of how much credit you hold. Manage all three under Additional usage credits at grok.com/dashboard/billing. On Teams, admins manage a shared prepaid balance and one cap for the whole team.

What happens when I reach my limit?

When your weekly pool runs out, you can:

Wait for the reset. Your pool refreshes weekly.

Use prepaid credit. Keep working at the same per-model rates with top-ups or Auto Top-up.

Upgrade your plan. Move to a higher tier for a larger weekly pool.

Requests are never downgraded in quality or speed. See Usage and limits for how to check usage and what the limit notification means.

Teams

Teams are priced per seat. Each member gets a Premium, Super, or Ultra seat at the same price and with the same weekly usage as the matching individual plan, and you can mix seat tiers on one team. Plus isn't available for teams.

Team plans add centralized billing and administration, a team marketplace for internal rules, skills, and plugins, usage analytics, team-wide privacy mode enforcement, custom Chat data retention, and SAML/OIDC SSO. Enterprise adds pooled usage across Grok and Cursor, invoicing, SCIM, granular spend controls, and advanced security controls.

We recommend Teams for any customer that is happy self-serving and Enterprise for customers that need priority support, pooled usage, invoicing, SCIM, or advanced security controls. Learn more about Team pricing.

Cursor Token Rate

On Teams and Enterprise plans, eligible third-party model requests include a Cursor Token Rate of $0.25 per million tokens. This rate applies on top of model API pricing for additional usage and BYOK usage. Individual plans don't pay it.

The rate applies when you select an eligible third-party model directly, and when Cursor Router (Balance or Intelligence) routes to one. It applies to input tokens, output tokens, and cached tokens.

The Cursor Token Rate does not apply to Cursor models, including Grok and Composer, or to Cost mode requests.

The Cursor Token Rate also does not apply to third-party models whose cache-read price is strictly below $0.10 per million tokens across every pricing tier. This exemption also applies when you use your own API key.

Auto modes

Auto has three modes: Cost, Balance, and Intelligence. See Cursor Router for how these modes work.

Cost mode (classic Auto) bills as a Cursor-model request and draws from your weekly pool whatever model serves it. On Teams and Enterprise with Cursor Router on, Balance and Intelligence bill at the routed model's list price: a route to a Cursor model draws from the weekly pool, and a route to a third-party model is third-party usage paid from prepaid credit. Individual plans have Cost mode only. See Model pricing for per-model rates. On Teams and Enterprise, a Balance or Intelligence route to a third-party model also incurs the Cursor Token Rate unless its cache-read price is strictly below $0.10 per million tokens across every pricing tier. Cost mode does not.

Legacy Cursor plans

Hobby, Start, Pro, Pro+, Ultra, and Teams Standard and Premium seats continue for existing customers. They include two monthly usage pools, Cursor Models and Other Models, and use on-demand usage instead of top-ups. See Legacy Cursor plans for how they work and how they map to The X Subscription.

Legacy request-based pricing

Max Mode

Max Mode is available only on legacy request-based plans. It extends a model's context window beyond the default limit and is billed at the model's API rate plus 20%. See Max Mode on legacy plans for details.

FAQ

Is my weekly pool shared with Grok?

Yes. Grok Chat, Imagine, Voice, Grok Bot, and Cursor all draw from the same weekly pool for Cursor models. Usage rates vary by product. The Grok API is billed separately and doesn't draw from the pool.

Can I use third-party models on Plus?

No. Plus includes Cursor's own models only (Grok 4.7, Grok 4.6, Grok 4.5, and Composer 2.5), at fixed settings. Upgrade to Premium or higher to use third-party models, billed at API list price from prepaid credit, and to choose Fast mode and effort levels.

Which models skip the Cursor Token Rate?

On Teams and Enterprise, third-party models whose cache-read price is strictly below $0.10 per million tokens across every pricing tier are exempt, including when you use your own API key. Cursor models and Cost mode requests are also exempt. Individual plans don't pay the rate.

Where are models hosted?

Models are hosted by the model provider, a trusted partner, or Cursor. See our list of sub-processors for details.

Where can I find pricing terms?

For enterprise pricing details, billing terms, and fee calculations, see the Pricing Policy.
