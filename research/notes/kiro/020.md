# 020 — Cost-Aware Contrastive Routing for LLMs (CSCR)
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12491 (NeurIPS 2025, University of Maryland et al., 2025-08)
- **Claim**: CSCR (Cost-Spectrum Contrastive Router) maps prompts and models into a shared embedding space and routes via a single k-NN lookup, using cost-aware contrastive training to favor the cheapest accurate expert; it improves the accuracy-cost tradeoff by up to 25% and generalizes to unseen models and OOD prompts with no retraining when the pool changes.
- **Mechanism**: Two gradient-free model fingerprints: (1) Logit footprints for open models — average probability over the K most frequent tokens across N probes and T decoding steps (<10 forward passes), ℓ2-normalized; (2) Perplexity fingerprints for black-box APIs — score each model's generated output with a shared small public LM (GPT-2), giving a per-prompt cross-entropy vector; both live on the unit sphere so one k-NN router can mix them. A frozen all-MiniLM-L6-v2 encoder + trainable 2-layer MLP projects prompts into the descriptor space. Training uses Cost-Spectrum InfoNCE: partition normalized costs into K percentile bands; keep ALL correct experts per band as positives (prevents cheap-expert collapse); band-specific temperature τ_k = τ_min + α·c̄_k (flatter softmax for costly/sparse bands); penalize negatives by their cost (subtract γ·c_m in denominator). Inference: retrieve top-k (k=4) via FAISS IndexFlatIP, pick argmax of cos_sim − λ·cost. No retraining when pool changes.
- **Numbers**: Up to 25% higher accuracy-cost efficiency on a fixed pool. Benchmarks: EmbedLLM, MixInstruct, RouterBench. Cost-aware vs vanilla InfoNCE on EmbedLLM: AUDC 0.495 vs 0.342, peak accuracy 54% vs 36%. AUDC results (CSCR vs best baselines): EmbedLLM 0.541 (UMR 0.515, Thompson 0.472); RouterBench 0.7110 (Parametric 0.691, Thompson 0.622); Oracle upper bounds 0.960/0.079/0.891. New-LLM generalization (EmbedLLM): CSCR AUDC 0.4848 vs UMR 0.4766. OOD (STEM→non-STEM): CSCR AUDC 0.5146 vs Thompson 0.4863. Hyperparams: K=5 cost bands, γ=0.2 (D.1 says 0.1), α=0.25, τ_min=0.05, k=4 NN, 192 probes, logit K=256 tokens, T=10, 256-d descriptor, 10 epochs, AdamW, batch 512, lr 5e-4. Descriptor ablation (MixInstruct): perplexity AUDC 0.467 vs logit 0.461 but perplexity ≥2× slower; mixed 0.473. Cost bands: 5 best (AUDC 0.548); 2/15/20 worse. RTX6000Ada ~48GB. Cost metric = params (EmbedLLM/MixInstruct) or real API USD (RouterBench). Theorem 5.2: excess risk ≤ C(√(k/n)+k^(−1/d)).
- **Token-cost implication**: Build cheap, reusable model fingerprints (logit footprints for open models, GPT-2 perplexity for APIs) and bake cost directly into the router's contrastive training objective rather than tuning cost only at inference—this prevents defaulting to either the biggest or the cheapest model, makes routing a microsecond k-NN lookup, and lets you add/remove models with no retraining.
- **Caveats**: Self-reported by authors (NeurIPS 2025). Could not test very large LLM pools due to resource limits. "Cost" for two of three benchmarks is parameter count as a proxy for compute/latency, not dollars. Perplexity fingerprint uses the same fixed scorer (GPT-2) and approximates true NLL. Minor hyperparameter inconsistency between main text (γ=0.2) and appendix (γ=0.1). Benchmarks dominated by low-cost experts, inflating random-baseline QNC.
