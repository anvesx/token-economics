# 006 — LLMRouterBench: A Massive Benchmark and Unified Framework for LLM Routing
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07206 (Shanghai AI Laboratory / arXiv, 2026-01)
- **Claim**: A large-scale unified benchmark (400K+ instances, 21 datasets, 33 models, 10 routing baselines) re-evaluates LLM routing and finds that under fair evaluation most routing methods perform nearly identically, several (including the commercial OpenRouter) fail to beat a simple Best-Single baseline, and a large gap to the Oracle persists due to model-recall failures.
- **Mechanism**: Two settings: performance-oriented (20 ~7B lightweight models, 15 datasets) and performance-cost (13 flagship models from 8 providers, 10 datasets). Modular framework = Collector (unified API, caching/retries/cost tracking), Evaluator (dataset-specific metrics), Adaptor (converts to algorithm-specific inputs, consistent train/test splits). Baselines: Random, Best Single, Oracle (lowest-cost correct model in hindsight) plus 10 routers (RouterDC, EmbedLLM, MODEL-SAT, GraphRouter, Avengers for performance; HybridLLM, FrugalGPT, RouteLLM, GraphRouter, Avengers-Pro, OpenRouter for perf-cost). Metrics: AvgAcc, Gain@R, Gain@B, Gap@O; PerfGain and CostSave vs Best Single; ParetoDist. Binary routers route between Qwen3-235B (weak) and GPT-5 (strong). Embedding methods use gte-Qwen2-7B-instruct. 70/30 split, 5 seeds (42,999,2024,2025,3407).
- **Numbers**: 400K+ instances; totals 23,945 prompts, 391,645 instances, ~1.8B tokens (perf-oriented 745.0M tokens, perf-cost 1,030.3M tokens); ~1K A800 GPU hours + $2,771.84 API cost (~$500 from LLM judging). ~17× more tokens than FusionFactory (1.8B vs 104M), ~3× more prompts than RouterArena (23,945 vs 8,400). Top routers achieve up to +4% AvgAcc over Best Single and up to 31.7% cost reduction. OpenRouter PerfGain = −24.7% vs Best Single (GPT-5). Model-recall failure example: on queries where ≤3 experts answer correctly (410 queries, 11.9% of test set) Avengers=24.6%, EmbedLLM=23.2% accuracy. Flagship prices ($/1M in→out): GPT-5 1.25→10, Claude-sonnet-4 3→15, Gemini-2.5-flash 0.30→2.50, Gemini-2.5-pro 1.25→10, Qwen3-235B 0.09→0.60, DeepSeek-R1 0.50→2.15, GLM-4.6 0.60→2.20, Kimi-K2 0.50→2.00, Intern-S1 0.18→0.54, DeepSeek-V3 0.25→0.88. Avengers uses k-means k=64; decoding temp 0.2, top_p 1.0; 10 retries. Embedding backbone ablation: nli-bert-base / all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (22.7M params) vs gte-Qwen2-7B show little difference (e.g. GraphRouter 69.60/68.05/70.29).
- **Token-cost implication**: Before adopting a fancy router, benchmark against Best Single and a cheap clustering router (Avengers/Avengers-Pro); gains are often attributable to coarse domain routing, so curate a small high-complementarity model pool rather than scaling the pool, and don't assume commercial auto-routers reduce cost without accuracy loss.
- **Caveats**: Self-reported benchmark by its authors; latency analysis is approximate (estimated from OpenRouter throughput, provider-specific, Jan 2026 snapshot). OpenRouter uses its own non-user-configurable pool, limiting comparability. Excludes domain-specific, very-long-context, and multimodal tasks. Does not cover all routing methods.
- **Topic**: routing
