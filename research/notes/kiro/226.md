# 226 — State of AI 2025: 100T Token LLM Usage Study
- **Source**: https://openrouter.ai/state-of-ai (OpenRouter, 2025-12)
- **Claim**: An empirical study of >100 trillion tokens of OpenRouter traffic finds reasoning models now exceed half of all usage, agentic/tool-calling and long-context workloads are rising, open-weight models reached ~1/3 of tokens, and demand is largely price-inelastic — quality/reliability trump token price, so the market is not yet commoditized.
- **Mechanism**: Analysis uses anonymized request-level metadata (no prompt text) for billions of prompt-completion pairs over ~13 months ending Nov 2025, with ~0.25% of prompts categorized by a GoogleTagClassifier (Google Cloud Natural Language classifyText, first 1,000 chars, confidence ≥0.5) mapped to study buckets (Programming, Roleplay, Translation, etc.). Open vs closed by weight availability; Chinese vs RoW by developer locale; geography by billing location. Cost analysis uses a blended effective price across prompt+completion tokens, accounts for caching, excludes BYOK. Agentic inference proxied via reasoning-model share, tool-call finish reasons, and sequence length. Retention measured as activity retention by monthly cohort ("Glass Slipper" / foundational cohort framework).
- **Numbers**: >100T tokens; 300+ models, 70+ providers; >50% of usage outside US. OSS share ~1/3 by late 2025; Chinese OSS averaged ~13.0% weekly (from 1.2% base, peaks ~30%), RoW OSS 13.7%, proprietary RoW ~70% avg. Top OSS authors (trillions of tokens Nov24–Nov25): DeepSeek 14.37, Qwen 5.59, Meta LLaMA 3.96, Mistral 2.92, OpenAI 1.65, Minimax 1.26, Z-AI 1.18, TNGTech 1.13, MoonshotAI 0.92, Google 0.82. No single OSS model >~20-25% of OSS tokens. Model sizes: Small <15B, Medium 15-70B, Large ≥70B. Reasoning-model token share: negligible early Q1 2025 → >50% late 2025. Roleplay ~52% of OSS tokens (58% of roleplay is RPG); Chinese OSS roleplay ~33%, programming+tech ~39%. Prompt tokens grew ~4× (1.5K→6K avg); completions ~3× (150→400); programming prompts routinely >20K input; avg sequence length <2,000 (late 2023) → >5,400 (late 2025). Programming queries ~11% of token volume early 2025 → >50% recent weeks; Anthropic >60% of programming spend (fell below 60% week of Nov 17); OpenAI programming share ~2%→~8% since July; Google ~15%. Geography: NA 47.22%, Asia 28.61% (up from ~13% to ~31% spend), Europe 21.32%, Oceania 1.18%, S.America 1.21%, Africa 0.46%. Top countries: US 47.17%, Singapore 9.21%, Germany 7.51%, China 6.01%. Languages: English 82.87%, Simplified Chinese 4.95%, Russian 2.47%, Spanish 1.43%. Price elasticity: 10% price drop → only ~0.5-0.7% usage increase. Median cost $0.73/1M tokens. Example model prices/usage(log): gemini-2.0-flash $0.147 / 6.68; deepseek-v3-0324 $0.394 / 6.55; claude-3.7-sonnet $1.963 / 6.87; claude-sonnet-4 $1.937 / 6.84; qwen-2-7b-instruct $0.052 / 2.91; gpt-4 $34.068 / 3.53; gpt-5-pro $34.965 / 3.42. Foundational cohorts (Gemini 2.5 Pro Jun 2025, Claude 4 Sonnet May 2025) retain ~40% of users at Month 5. o1 released Dec 5 2024 (preview Sep 12 2024).
- **Token-cost implication**: Build model-agnostic, multi-model stacks and route by task: cheap efficient models (Gemini Flash, DeepSeek V3 ~<$0.40/1M) for high-volume/long-context, premium models (Claude, GPT) for high-stakes work where cost is negligible vs outcome value. Expect agentic/long-context to dominate — programming prompts >20K input tokens — so context and caching management is the main cost lever. Because demand is price-inelastic, optimize for cost-per-successful-outcome, not cost-per-token; cheap-but-unreliable models fail to win integration. Watch retention/foundational cohorts, not raw growth, as the durable-adoption signal.
- **Caveats**: Single platform (OpenRouter), observational, ~13-month window; excludes enterprise/local/internal deployments and BYOK. Categories only from ~0.25% sampled prompts via an external classifier (predefined taxonomy, first 1,000 chars), available only since ~May 2025. Geography inferred from billing, not verified location. Agentic use is a proxy (tool-call finish reason / sequence length). Some spikes attributed to single large accounts; xAI usage distorted by a free-access period. Co-authored by a16z + OpenRouter (platform operator) — not independent of the data source.
