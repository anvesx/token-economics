# 090 — Efficient Reasoning via Chain of Unconscious Thought
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19756 (arXiv / HUST, NUS, NTU, Moonshot AI, Zhejiang Normal, 2025-05)
- **Claim**: Chain of Unconscious Thought (CoUT) is a training-free prompting paradigm, inspired by Unconscious Thought Theory, that tells large reasoning models to reason internally in hidden layers and emit minimal output, cutting token usage while keeping accuracy comparable to Chain-of-Thought.
- **Mechanism**: Two components. (1) Reasoning Process Internalization (RPI): prompt the model to "Process and solve problems fully in your hidden layer thinking. Output bare minimum answers with only single-line reasoning when necessary," leveraging latent/"unconscious" computation instead of externalizing every step. (2) Token-Efficient Strategies (TES): a prompt bag — "TOKEN CONSERVATION MODE ACTIVE," use symbols/abbreviations (&, w/, =, →), omit articles, strip non-essential words, and a scoring frame ("Each saved token equals +1 efficiency point while each accuracy error costs -100 efficiency points," "maximum precision with minimum verbosity"). Combined into one CoUT prompt. Compared against CoT, CoD (Chain-of-Draft, ≤5 words/step), CCoT (≤45 tokens), TALE-EP (two-stage token-budget prediction).
- **Numbers**: Reduces token usage by 47.62% vs CoT while maintaining comparable accuracy (abstract); elsewhere stated as 20.51% reduction with only 0.1% accuracy drop (vs runner-up average). Arithmetic (GSM8K+SVAMP avg): CoUT 190.19 tokens (≈88.7% less than TALE-EP 1680.09; ≈39.0% less than CCoT 311.76), accuracy 94.28% vs CoT 93.50%, CoD 79.51%, CCoT 91.63%. Mathematical (MathQA+AQuA avg): CoUT 518.73 tokens (>100 fewer than CCoT 580.08), accuracy 82.53% vs CoD 82.27%. Ablation: RPI alone 88.61% acc / 466.40 tokens (−31.1% vs CoT); TES alone 87.38% / 447.28 (−33.9%); CoUT both 88.40% / 354.46 tokens (−47.6% vs CoT 676.85), accuracy drop limited to 2.79% (91.19%→88.40%). Models: GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, O3-mini, QwQ-32B; zero-shot. Datasets: GSM8K (1319), SVAMP (1000), AQuA (254), MathQA (2985).
- **Token-cost implication**: For reasoning tasks, a purely prompt-side change (internalize reasoning + aggressive output-compression instructions) can roughly halve output/reasoning tokens with negligible accuracy loss, no training or extra models — a cheap, portable lever to cut per-query decode cost on LRMs. Symbol use, article omission, and an explicit token-vs-accuracy scoring frame are the concrete prompt ingredients.
- **Caveats**: Self-reported academic results; the headline reduction varies in the paper (47.62% vs 20.51%) depending on comparison/aggregation. Ineffective on QwQ-32B (token count stays high regardless of prompt). Only math/arithmetic benchmarks tested; no few-shot, no larger models, no code/commonsense tasks (left to future work). Authors note very low token output may raise safety concerns for LRMs.
- **Topic**: reasoning-budget
