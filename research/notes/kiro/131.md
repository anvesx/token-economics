# 131 — s1: Simple test-time scaling
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19393 (arXiv, 2025-01)
- **Claim**: Shows that supervised fine-tuning on only 1,000 carefully curated reasoning samples plus a simple decoding-time trick called "budget forcing" produces a reasoning model (s1-32B) that exhibits test-time scaling and is competitive with OpenAI o1-preview.
- **Mechanism**: Curate s1K (1,000 questions filtered from 59K by quality, difficulty, diversity) with reasoning traces distilled from Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking; SFT Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct on it. Control test-time compute via "budget forcing": to cap thinking, append the end-of-thinking delimiter (and optionally "Final Answer:"); to extend thinking, suppress the end-of-thinking delimiter and append "Wait" (2x/4x/6x) to force continued reasoning and self-correction. Sequential scaling (longer single trace) is argued to beat parallel (majority voting); REBASE process-reward tree search scales even better than majority voting.
- **Numbers**: Training: 1,000 samples, 5 epochs, batch 16, 315 gradient steps, LR 1e-5, 26 minutes on 16 NVIDIA H100 GPUs (7 H100 GPU-hours; vs 394 H100 GPU-hours for the 59K-full set). Budget forcing improves AIME24 from 50% to 57% (one claim "up to 27%" over o1-preview on competition math). s1-32B: MATH500 93.0, GPQA Diamond 59.6, AIME24 56.7 (BF ~30k thinking-token cap). r1-distill trained on 800x more reasoning samples (800K). GPQA Diamond: PhD experts score 69.7%. Budget-forcing control metric = 100% (perfect); rejection sampling shows inverse scaling (avg 655/97/8/3/2/1 tries per sample for ≤3500/4000/5000/8000/16000 token caps). s1.1 (traces regenerated with DeepSeek r1): MATH500 95.4, AIME24 up to 56.7. s1K correctness 53.6% (s1K-1.1 63.0%). Full 59K pool gives no substantial gain over 1K.
- **Token-cost implication**: Reasoning ability can be cheaply "activated" with ~1K SFT samples rather than hundreds of thousands; test-time compute (thinking tokens) is a tunable knob — you can trade accuracy against token spend with a dead-simple intervention ("Wait"), and scaling down compute behaves predictably. Budget forcing gives perfect controllability over thinking-token spend, unlike telling the model a token budget in the prompt (which it cannot reliably count).
- **Caveats**: Academic, self-reported; budget forcing flattens out after ~6x extrapolation and is bounded by the model's context window; too much suppression causes repetitive loops. Evaluation is non-deterministic even with greedy sampling (vLLM batch-size/tensor-parallelism effects) so they use full precision. Distilled from Gemini 2.0, so gains partly reflect distillation. r1-32B is stronger but uses 800x more data.
