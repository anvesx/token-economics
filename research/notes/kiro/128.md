# 128 — 1 Billion Classifications
- **Source**: https://huggingface.co/blog/billion-classifications (Hugging Face, 2025-02)
- **Claim**: A methodology blog for costing and optimizing large-scale (1B+/day) encoder inference — classification, text embedding, and vision embedding — by sweeping batch size, virtual users, GPU type, and serving image, and reporting the cheapest config for processing 1 billion inputs.
- **Mechanism**: Deploy encoder models on HF Inference Endpoints across GPU options (T4, L4); serve with the Infinity library (also TEI); load-test with k6 Grafana using the shared-iterations executor (10,000 requests, 1 min max per experiment); sweep INFINITY_BATCH_SIZE [16–1024] and VUs via exponential increase then binary search (2% improvement threshold); scale a single-GPU baseline horizontally by replica count. Cost of 1B inputs is extrapolated from measured throughput and GPU $/hr.
- **Numbers**: Cost of 1B inputs — Classification (distilbert-base-multilingual-cased-sentiments-student, 135M, DistilBERT) $253.82 on nvidia-L4 ($0.8/hr), Infinity default image, batch_size 64, vus 448. Embedding (gte-modernbert-base, 149M, ModernBERT) $409.44 on L4, default image, batch_size 32, vus 256, data avg 144 tokens (std 14). Vision-Embedding (colqwen2-v1.0-merged, 2.21B, ColQwen2 ColBERT-style) $44,496.51 on L4, batch_size 4, vus 4, images ~600x400 at 4MB. GPU prices: nvidia-L4 $0.8/hr, nvidia-T4 $0.5/hr. Classification data avg 100 tokens (min 50). Vision embedding is ~2 orders of magnitude (over $44,000 per 1B) costlier than text; 2B model is ~15x bigger than the other models. Classification accuracy cited 58% (deemed irrelevant, only a sanity check).
- **Token-cost implication**: For batch/bulk encoder workloads, L4 beats T4 and CPUs at scale; maximize batch size up to GPU memory/bandwidth limits (optimal 64 for classification, 32 for embeddings) and tune VUs to saturate; expect ColBERT-style vision embeddings to cost ~100x text, so avoid them unless retrieval quality justifies it. Use the official Infinity client (base64, smaller payloads) for production rather than k6.
- **Caveats**: Self-reported by the author on HF Inference Endpoints; results not treated as exactly repeatable (slight variation across identical runs). Costs are extrapolations, not full 1B runs. Numbers depend on the author's specific data, models, hardware, and HF pricing; the author explicitly says to re-run on your own task. Deploying multiple models on one GPU gave no major improvement (bandwidth-bound).
