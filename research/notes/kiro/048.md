# 048 — ATLAS: All-round Testing of Long-context Abilities across Scales

- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28079 (arXiv / Meituan + Fudan University, 2026-05)
- **Claim**: Long-context LLM evaluation should be a length-dependent capability profile, not a single-length or single-task score. ATLAS measures 8 capability dimensions across a fixed 8K–1M length grid with length-aware AUC scoring and a harmonic-mean aggregate (ATLAScore), revealing that model rankings reshuffle with length scope and that retrieval skill doesn't transfer to downstream use.
- **Mechanism**: A layered "3+5" taxonomy: foundational layer (retrieval, aggregation, multi-step reasoning) whose answers are input-determined, plus application layer (QA, in-context learning, code understanding, long-range memory, holistic assessment). Each length-sliceable dimension is scored at geometric length slices L = {8K,16K,32K,64K,128K,256K,512K,1M} and integrated via normalized trapezoidal AUC up to a reporting scope (128K or 1M); geometric spacing naturally up-weights longer intervals. Three category aggregates (foundational B, length-sliced application C, holistic S at original lengths) are combined by harmonic mean, which penalizes imbalanced profiles. Uncertainty is propagated end-to-end (CLT/cluster/weighted subset CIs → linear AUC propagation → delta-method through the nonlinear harmonic mean, Monte-Carlo validated). Components are replaceable instantiations chosen by length-extensibility, deterministic scoring, and cross-model discrimination, validated by leave-one-out.
- **Numbers**: 8 dimensions, 9 components, 6,438 instances, 26 models evaluated. Gemini-3.1-Pro-Preview leads at 128K; Claude-Opus-4.6 leads at 1M; GPT-5.5 third in both. Rank reshuffle 128K→1M: 20/26 models shift, 7 by ≥2 ranks; GPT-5.2 drops 4 ranks (4th→8th, losing >15 points). Mean relative decay (128K→1M) 24.3%; lowest Claude-Opus-4.6 8.5%, highest GLM-4.7 (non-reasoning) 60.5%. Foundational vs application layers share only 61% of cross-model variance at 128K (R²=0.61, ρ=0.74); at 1M R²=0.73, ρ=0.88. Layer-rank gaps |Δrank|≥4 for 15 models at 128K (max 12 positions, e.g. Kimi-K2.6 foundational 18th but application 6th); 11 models at 1M (max 7). Dimension decay: Retrieval/QA most fragile (some >70%, e.g. GLM-4.7 Retrieval 82.2%, QA 84.8%); Code most independent/stable (ρ with Retrieval 0.07, Reasoning 0.17; σ=8.9 at 128K). GPT-5.2 ICL 97.96 at 1M but Retrieval decays 46.4% (88.21→47.31), QA 59.2%. Mean pairwise Spearman ρ̄=0.64 at 128K. 11/15 reasoning models decay <25% vs non-reasoning 19.8–60.5%. Leave-one-dimension-out ρ≥0.97 (128K), ≥0.96 (1M). 95% CIs typically 1–2 points. ATLAS-Lite (128K-only) ~14.5% of token cost, ρ=0.977 vs full ranking; full protocol ~1,711M tokens/model (1M slice ~45%).
- **Token-cost implication**: Don't trust a single advertised context window or one benchmark length — effective long-context ability is length- and capability-specific, so pick models by the profile (and length scope) matching your workload (e.g., high ICL ≠ good retrieval at 1M). For cost-constrained screening, a 128K-only ATLAS-Lite recovers most of the ranking at ~1/7 the token cost, but reserve the full 8K–1M protocol for deployments beyond 128K since it alone catches post-128K cliffs.
- **Caveats**: Self-reported (Meituan/Fudan). Benchmark design/framework paper, not a model contribution. English-only; holistic assessment evaluated at original lengths (not length-sliced); AMemBench-ACU uses model-generated transcripts (acknowledged distribution-match bias toward similar models); LongCodeBench starts at 32K; open-ended summarization omitted (unreliable scoring). Reasoning-vs-decay is a correlation under deployed settings, not causal. Public components need periodic refresh against contamination. Many model names appear to be future/hypothetical versions (e.g., GPT-5.x, Gemini-3.x, Claude-Opus-4.6).
