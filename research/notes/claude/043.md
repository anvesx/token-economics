# 043 — Bringing the Artificial Analysis LLM Performance Leaderboard to Hugging Face
- **Source**: https://huggingface.co/blog/leaderboard-artificial-analysis (Hugging Face / Artificial Analysis, 2024-05-03)
- **Claim**: Artificial Analysis publishes a leaderboard comparing quality, price, throughput and latency across >100 serverless LLM API endpoints, and argues multi-request designs with cheaper, faster models can cut cost and even improve quality.
- **Mechanism**: Each endpoint is tested 8 times per day; figures are the median of the prior 14 days, with P5/P25/P75/P95 breakdowns. Workloads: prompt lengths ~100, ~1k, ~10k tokens, times 1 or 10 parallel queries (6 combinations). Blended price assumes a 3:1 input:output token ratio. Quality is a simplified index from MMLU, MT-Bench, HumanEval as reported by model authors, plus Chatbot Arena ranking. Throughput = tokens/s, latency = time to first token.
- **Numbers**: >100 endpoints; 300x pricing spread "from Claude 3 Opus to Llama 3 8B" (May 2024); 7 providers served Llama 3 within 48 hours; blended price 3:1 input:output. Example (no figures): reading dozens of pages with Llama 3 8B in parallel then summarizing with GPT-4 Turbo is "more cost effective, even after accounting for reading 10x more content".
- **Token-cost implication**: Compare models on blended price plus speed, not quality alone; delegate high-volume reading/extraction to a small fast model and reserve the large model for the final judgment step. Check the blended-price ratio against your own input:output mix.
- **Caveats**: Dated May 2024 prices and models. Quality scores are model-author-reported at that time, not independently measured per endpoint. The cheaper-pipeline example is an illustration without measured results. Vendor/aggregator blog.
- **Topic**: pricing
