# 110 — Taming Throughput-Latency Tradeoff in LLM Inference with Sarathi-Serve
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02310 (Microsoft Research India / Georgia Tech, Agrawal et al., 2024-03; v3 June 2024)
- **Claim**: Splitting prefills into chunks and building "stall-free" batches that add a bounded number of prefill tokens to ongoing decodes lets a server run large batches (high throughput) without long generation stalls (low tail time-between-tokens), and also reduces pipeline bubbles.
- **Mechanism**: Each iteration has a fixed token budget. Schedule all ongoing decodes first, then fill the leftover budget with chunks of prefill from waiting requests (hybrid batches). Iteration latency becomes nearly independent of prompt length, and micro-batches under pipeline parallelism get near-uniform compute so bubbles shrink. The token budget is the control knob: small budget (512) for strict TBT limits, large (2048) for relaxed limits; chosen by one-time profiling and the Vidur simulator, tied to the TBT SLO and chunking overhead. Prefill throughput saturates at about 512 tokens.
- **Numbers**: Serving capacity under P99 TBT SLOs vs vLLM: 2.6x (Mistral-7B, 1 A100) and up to 3.7x (Yi-34B, 2 A100 with TP2) under strict SLO; up to 5.6x end-to-end for Falcon-180B with pipeline parallelism (8 A100, TP4 x PP2, 100 Gbps Ethernet). Under strict SLO: up to 4.0x vs Orca and 3.7x vs vLLM (Yi-34B, openchat_sharegpt4); up to 6.3x vs Orca and 4.3x vs vLLM (LLaMA2-70B). 3.5x higher capacity than vLLM at 100 ms SLO (Mistral-7B, token budget 512); 1.65x at a 1 s SLO (Yi-34B, token budget 2048). Strict and relaxed SLO = 5x and 25x a decode iteration time (4k prefill, batch 32). Token budgets used: 2048 relaxed, 512 strict (1536 for LLaMA2-70B relaxed). Decode batches with a full prefill show up to 28.3x TBT latency increase in the paper's example vs a decode-only batch. Chunking overhead: at most about 25% of prefill runtime at chunk 512; negligible at 2048. Pure TP-8 across nodes had about 2x the median latency of TP4-PP2. Median prompt lengths: 7059 tokens (arxiv_summarization) vs 1730 (openchat_sharegpt4).
- **Token-cost implication**: Serving cost per token drops when you can run large batches without breaking latency targets. If self-hosting, use a stack with chunked prefill and stall-free scheduling and tune the per-iteration token budget to your TBT SLO. Long-prompt workloads (thousands of tokens) benefit most. Prefer TP within a node and PP across nodes.
- **Caveats**: Authors' own evaluation (the system is theirs) on A100/A40 GPUs with Mistral-7B, Yi-34B, LLaMA2-70B and Falcon-180B and two datasets; baselines are vLLM and Orca of early 2024 (vLLM later added chunked prefill; not tested). Capacity is defined at a P99 TBT SLO with a 2 s median scheduling delay limit. Token budget is static; dynamic budgets were left for future work. Compared with disaggregated systems (Splitwise, DistServe) only in discussion. Plot data garbled in the extraction.
