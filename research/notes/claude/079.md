# 079 — LLMLingua: Compressing Prompts for Accelerated Inference of Large Language Models
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05736 (Microsoft Research, 2023-10)
- **Claim**: A small language model can drop low-information tokens from a long prompt before it is sent to a black-box LLM, giving up to 20x prompt compression with little loss on reasoning and summarization tasks. It needs no gradients through the target LLM, so it works with API-only models.
- **Mechanism**: Three parts. (1) Budget controller: splits the prompt into instruction, demonstrations and question. Instruction and question get light compression (pre-set rates tau_ins = 0.85, tau_que = 0.9). Demonstrations are ranked by small-LM perplexity and whole demonstrations are kept until the budget (k * tau_dems * L_dems, k = 2) is hit; leftover budget goes back to instruction and question. (2) Iterative token-level compression: the prompt is cut into segments (size 100), the small LM computes token perplexity conditioned on the already-compressed earlier segments, and tokens above a per-segment threshold are kept, which models dependencies between kept tokens. (3) Distribution alignment: the small LM (Alpaca-7B or GPT2-Alpaca) is instruction-tuned on LLM-generated data so its perplexity matches the target LLM. Compression cost model: c = (L + kL/tau + L/tau) * c_small + (L/tau) * c_LLM.
- **Numbers**: Target LLMs GPT-3.5-Turbo-0301 and Claude-v1.3, greedy decoding. GSM8K full-shot 78.85 EM at 2,366 tokens. LLMLingua at 1-shot constraint: 79.08 EM, 446 tokens, 5x. Half-shot: 77.41 EM, 171 tokens, 14x. Quarter-shot: 77.33 EM, 117 tokens, 20x. Selective-Context at quarter-shot 44.20 EM (157 tokens, 15x); GPT-4 generation 56.33 EM (188 tokens, 20x). BBH full-shot 70.07 EM; LLMLingua 70.11 EM at 3x, 61.60 at 7x, 56.85 at 7x (the quarter-shot row; the table is partly garbled in extraction). ShareGPT and Arxiv-March23: up to 9x and 3.3x compression with high BERTScore F1 (about 89-90). Claude-v1.3 on GSM8K: 83.51 EM at 5x and 82.61 at 14x, vs 81.8 for the simple prompt at 691 tokens. GPT2-Alpaca as small LM: 77.02 EM at 5x, 76.42 at 14x, 76.27 at 18x (about 1-2 EM below Alpaca-7B). Ablation on GSM8K 1-shot: full 79.08; without iterative token-level compression 72.93; without budget controller 73.62; random demo selection 72.78; without distribution alignment 78.62. Latency on V100-32G, GSM8K: end-to-end 8.6 s without compression; with LLMLingua 4.9 s at 2x (1.7x speedup), 2.3 s at 5x (3.3x), 1.3 s at 10x (5.7x); compression itself takes 0.8, 0.3, 0.2 s. Estimated compute at 5x compression is about 0.264 * L * c_LLM, nearly 4x saving, assuming c_small is 1/25 of c_LLM. GPT-3.5-Turbo inference cost in dollars, original vs compressed: GSM8K 5.2 vs 0.5, BBH 12.8 vs 4.8, ShareGPT 0.7 vs 0.3, Arxiv 1.3 vs 0.2. Generated output length also fell as compression rose (Figure 2).
- **Token-cost implication**: For long few-shot or CoT prompts sent to a paid API, run a small local LM to prune demonstrations and tokens first. Keep instruction and question mostly intact and compress demonstrations hardest. Budget for the small-LM overhead (0.2-0.8 s here). Compressed text is unreadable to humans; log the original for debugging.
- **Caveats**: Authors' own benchmarks (GSM8K, BBH, ShareGPT, Arxiv-March23) on GPT-3.5-Turbo and Claude-v1.3, both old models. Token counts via tiktoken for GPT-3.5. Accuracy degrades sharply at 25x-30x on GSM8K. Dollar costs are estimates from GPT-3.5-Turbo pricing at the time, not measured invoices. BBH dropped 8.5 to 13.2 EM points at the higher ratios. A tokenizer mismatch between the small LM and target LLM can understate token length. Latency measured on a single V100 only.
