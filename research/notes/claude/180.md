# 180 — The Total Cost of AI Ownership
- **Source**: https://cohere.com/blog/blog/the-total-cost-of-ai-ownership (Cohere, 2026-07-15)
- **Claim**: Token price is only the visible part of AI cost; utilization decides whether owning or renting inference is cheaper, and owning wins decisively for always-on workloads.
- **Mechanism**: One user request fans out into long prompts, large context, reasoning steps, tool calls, retries and retrieval, and the company pays for the whole chain. Cost levers named: model routing to right-sized models, mixture-of-experts, low-bit quantization, and high utilization of owned hardware. Owned hardware turns cost into a fixed depreciating expense with near-zero marginal cost per request; idle hardware is waste.
- **Numbers**: Gartner projects global AI spending of $2.52 trillion USD in 2026, up 44%. IDC/DataRobot (Dec 2025): 96% of genAI deployers and 92% of agentic AI users saw higher-than-expected costs. McKinsey (Nov 2025, ~2,000 orgs, 105 countries): about one-third scaling enterprise-wide, 5-6% report significant financial impact. Mavvrik/Benchmarkit (2025): 80% miss AI infrastructure forecasts by over 25%, nearly 25% miss by more than 50%, only 15% within 10%; 84% report gross-margin erosion of 6% or more. Uber example: 12 months of AI budget spent in four. Lenovo 2026 analysis (8-GPU servers, 70B-405B models): about $0.11 per million tokens on owned H100, about $0.89 cloud instance, around $2.00 frontier API (8x vs cloud, up to 18x vs API); server pays back in under four months versus on-demand cloud; owning beats renting at roughly four hours of use per day. NVIDIA: $0.123 per million tokens on GB300 (SemiAnalysis InferenceX); Blackwell-class roughly 50x more tokens per megawatt than Hopper, about 35x lower cost per token. Illustrative workload: half a million requests a day, a few thousand tokens each.
- **Token-cost implication**: Track tokens per task, model calls per action, retries and context size, not just $/M tokens. For steady always-on inference, model the break-even utilization of owned or dedicated hardware against API pricing.
- **Caveats**: Vendor marketing from Cohere (sells private deployments); the cost figures are Lenovo and NVIDIA vendor numbers on their own hardware, the cloud side omits storage, egress and support, the Lenovo and NVIDIA figures are not strictly comparable, and all assume sustained high utilization.
