# 278 — Dedicated endpoints pricing (Together AI)
- **Source**: https://docs.together.ai/docs/dedicated-endpoints/pricing (Together AI, 2026-10)
- **Claim**: Together AI dedicated model inference (DMI) bills per minute per ready replica by hardware, not by tokens or model, and the page explains when this beats per-token serverless.
- **Mechanism**: Billing is per minute, per replica, only while a replica is ready (provisioning, cold start and DEGRADED time are not billed); scaled-to-zero or stopped deployments cost nothing. minReplicas sets the cost floor, maxReplicas the ceiling; an inactivity timeout can stop idle deployments. Multi-GPU configs cost proportionally to the single-GPU rate. Two options: on-demand (no commitment) and reserved (committed term, lower effective rate, guaranteed availability). Compare by computing DMI monthly cost versus monthly tokens times serverless per-token price.
- **Numbers**: H100 80GB 1x: $5.49 per hour; B200 180GB 1x: $8.99 per hour; H200, GB300, B300: contact sales. One H100 replica running continuously is about $132 per day, roughly $3,950 per 30-day month. Four-GPU config costs four times the single-GPU rate.
- **Token-cost implication**: Dedicated is cheaper only when replicas stay busy most of the day; set minReplicas to the lowest count meeting the latency target, scale to zero or use inactivity timeout when idle, and use serverless for low or bursty traffic. Steady low-volume 24/7 traffic is the worst case for dedicated.
- **Caveats**: Vendor list prices as of 2026-10; reserved rates not published; no throughput or tokens-per-GPU figures, so break-even needs your own measurement.
