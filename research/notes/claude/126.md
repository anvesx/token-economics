# 126 — Announcing AI Energy Score Ratings
- **Source**: https://huggingface.co/blog/sasha/announcing-ai-energy-score (Hugging Face community blog, Sasha Luccioni, 2025-02-11)
- **Claim**: Launches the AI Energy Score project: a standardized benchmark, public leaderboard and 1-5 star rating for the energy consumption of AI models on specific tasks.
- **Mechanism**: A uniform framework measures model energy use per task. Open models are tested automatically; closed models are evaluated through a secure Docker environment for reproducibility. Models earn 1 to 5 stars per task, with the most efficient getting five. Developers can submit models through a benchmarking portal.
- **Numbers**: Leaderboard ranks 166 models across 10 common AI tasks (text and image generation, summarization, etc.), including LLaMa, Phi, Gemma, Mistral, SmolLM. Star scale 1 to 5. No energy (Wh) or dollar figures in the text.
- **Token-cost implication**: Little direct dollar impact. Energy per task is a usable extra selection criterion when choosing smaller models, and a proxy for compute cost.
- **Caveats**: Announcement only; no measured values. Self-reported by the project authors. Proprietary models covered only if submitted. A commenter notes smolLM-135m and smolLM-1.7B score nearly the same despite a 10x size difference and that encoder-decoder models are not supported (unanswered in the text).
