# 049 — LiveBench: A Challenging, Contamination-Free LLM Benchmark
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19314 (Abacus.AI with NYU, Nvidia, UMD, USC, Columbia; ICLR 2025, 2024-06; v2 18 Apr 2025)
- **Claim**: LiveBench is a benchmark of objectively scored questions drawn from recent sources and refreshed monthly, so models are unlikely to have seen the questions in training. No LLM judge is used.
- **Mechanism**: 18 tasks in 6 categories (math, coding, reasoning, language, instruction following, data analysis) built from recent math competitions, arXiv papers, news and datasets, plus harder versions of Big-Bench Hard, AMPS and IFEval tasks (16-instruction IFEval subset). Every question has a verifiable ground-truth answer scored automatically. About 1/6 of questions are replaced per update so the set is fully refreshed roughly every 6 months. Category score = mean of its tasks; overall score = mean of the six categories. Single-turn evaluation at temperature 0, with each model's own chat template.
- **Numbers**: 40 LLMs evaluated, from 0.5B to 405B parameters. Top models score below 70% accuracy (abstract). o1-preview-2024-09-12 is best overall, 6% better than all other models; claude-3-5-sonnet-20240620 is next and far ahead in coding; o1-mini-2024-09-12 is third and strongest in reasoning. Best open models: llama-3.1-405b-instruct and qwen2.5-72b-instruct, roughly tied and above gpt-4-turbo. Rank correlation between original and first update, and first and second update, is > 0.997. Mean and median scores dropped about 1.2% between the original and latest question sets. First update: benchmark size became 1000 questions; second update replaced 132 questions. No prices or costs.
- **Token-cost implication**: When choosing a cheaper model for routing or cascades, use a contamination-limited benchmark such as LiveBench for quality estimates, because static benchmark scores can be inflated by training-set leakage. It does not measure cost, so pair it with price per token yourself.
- **Caveats**: Run by the benchmark's authors on the models they selected; model scores come from their own harness. Only tasks with objective answers are covered, so open-ended writing tasks are out. The authors note prompt-style biases among model families and plan prompt updates. Contamination is limited, not eliminated, and older questions age as they are published. Scores in this paper are from late 2024 and are dated.
