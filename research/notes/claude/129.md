# 129 — Investing in Performance: Fine-tune small models with LLM insights (CFM case study)
- **Source**: https://huggingface.co/blog/cfm-case-study (Hugging Face blog with Capital Fund Management, 2024-12-03)
- **Claim**: For financial-news company-name NER, using Llama 3.1 70B to pre-label data and then fine-tuning small encoders (GLiNER, SpanMarker) gives accuracy close to the large LLM at far lower inference cost, stated as up to 80x cheaper.
- **Mechanism**: (1) Deploy Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct on HF Inference Endpoints (4x Nvidia L40S, Text Generation Inference) with a role + instructions + JSON schema (Pydantic, guided output) + few-shot prompt, temperature 0.1, max_tokens 512, async requests in batches of 128 over ~900k Benzinga headlines (FNSPID). (2) Cluster predicted company names (rapidfuzz Levenshtein, threshold 85), sample 5% per cluster and 5% of no-company headlines: 2,714 samples split train 2,405 (2010-2016), validation 204 (2017-2018), test 105 (2019-2020). (3) Human review of LLM pre-labels in Argilla (5 to 10 s per sample vs about 30 s raw). (4) Fine-tune GLiNER-medium-news and SpanMarker on one A10 GPU (GLiNER: 20 epochs, batch 4, lr 5e-6, pick best validation F1). (5) Serve small models on GPU or CPU.
- **Numbers**: Labeling 900k samples with Llama 3.1 70B: about 8 hours, about $70. Annotating 2,714 samples: about 8 hours. Zero-shot F1 on the 2,714 reviewed set: GLiNER 87%, SpanMarker 47%, Llama 3.1 8B 88%, Llama 3.1 70B 95%. Test-set F1 (105 samples): GLiNER fine-tuned 93.4% (from 87.0%), SpanMarker fine-tuned 90.1% (from 47.0%), Llama 3.1 8B 80.0%, Llama 3.1 70B 92.7%. Inference cost per hour: Llama 70B at least $8, Llama 8B $4.00 (summary table), small models about $0.50 (GPU, 16x cheaper) or about $0.10 (CPU, 80x cheaper). 1,000 samples: manual labeling 3 hours at F1 0.915 vs Llama 70B 2 minutes at F1 0.895 (GLiNER trained on each). Model sizes: GLiNER small 50M, medium 90M, large 0.3B parameters. Headline claim of up to 6.4% accuracy improvement and 80x cheaper.
- **Token-cost implication**: For narrow, high-volume extraction/classification, use a large LLM once to label a dataset, then distill into a small fine-tuned model and stop paying per-token LLM inference for that task.
- **Caveats**: Vendor/partner case study, self-reported. Test set only 105 samples (Llama 8B scores 88% on the full reviewed set but 80% on test; a commenter says this variability undermines the claimed gain). Cost is per instance-hour, not per task: no throughput or latency comparison, so "80x cheaper" may not hold per request (commenter's point). Single task, English finance headlines. Human-labeled data beat LLM-labeled data at larger sizes.
