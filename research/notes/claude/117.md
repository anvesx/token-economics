# 117 — Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs? A Comprehensive Study and Hybrid Approach
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16833 (Google DeepMind, 2024-05)
- **Claim**: Benchmarking RAG against long-context (LC) prompting on 9 QA/summarization datasets, LC beats RAG on average when the model has enough context window, but RAG is far cheaper. SELF-ROUTE lets the model itself decide per query whether RAG suffices, cutting tokens while matching LC quality.
- **Mechanism**: Step 1 (RAG-and-Route): give the LLM the query plus top-k retrieved chunks (300-word chunks, default k=5, Contriever or Dragon, cosine similarity) with the instruction "Write unanswerable if the query can not be answered based on the provided text". If it answers, accept. Step 2: if it says unanswerable, resend the query with the full context to the long-context LLM. Relies on the model being calibrated about answerability. Cost proxy is input token count relative to LC.
- **Numbers**: Contriever, avg over 9 datasets. Gemini-1.5-Pro: LC 49.70, RAG 37.33, SELF-ROUTE 46.41, answerable 76.78%, tokens 38.39% of LC. GPT-4O: LC 48.67, RAG 32.60, SELF-ROUTE 48.89, answerable 57.36%, tokens 61.40%. GPT-3.5-Turbo: LC 32.07, RAG 30.33, SELF-ROUTE 35.32, answerable 74.10%, tokens 38.85%. Stated cost reduction: 65% for Gemini-1.5-Pro, 39% for GPT-4O. LC over RAG on average: +7.6% Gemini, +13.1% GPT-4O, +3.6% GPT-3.5-Turbo. RAG and LC predictions identical for 63% of queries; score gap under 10 for 70%. k ablation (Gemini, LC=45.53): k=1 RAG 20.24, SELF-ROUTE 41.35 at token ratio 5.26 / 39.64; k=5 RAG 37.92, SELF-ROUTE 43.33 at 17.02 / 38.63; k=10 41.20, 44.38 at 42.42 / 53.66; k=50 44.06, 45.19 at 95.29 / 102.97. Cost is lowest at k=5 on average; k=1 is cheapest on extractive datasets like NarrativeQA and QMSum. Dataset lengths: LongBench avg about 7k words, InfinityBench En.QA/En.MC about 150k and 143k words. Synthetic PassKey: RAG 80.34 vs LC 65.25; with "special token" wording RAG 4.58 vs LC 69.32.
- **Token-cost implication**: Put a cheap RAG pass in front of the long-context call and escalate only on "unanswerable"; expect roughly 40-60% of LC input tokens. Tune k per workload (k around 5 as default) since larger k raises both RAG and routing cost. Use RAG outright when the document exceeds the model's context window (GPT-3.5-Turbo 16k beat LC on En.QA/En.MC).
- **Caveats**: Authors' own results, no independent replication. Cost is measured as input-token percentage, not billed dollars; retrieval and embedding cost excluded (footnote says retrieval is easy to host customer-side). Only GPT-3.5-Turbo, GPT-4O (2024-05-13) and Gemini-1.5-Pro tested. Answerable rate differs by model: OpenAI models reject more often (lower answerable %, higher accuracy). Routing failures noted for multi-step, general, long/complex and implicit queries. Possible training-data leakage; mitigated with a "based only on the provided passage" prompt, which lowered Gemini LC avg from 50.57 to 45.53. Synthetic benchmarks show dataset artifacts. Latency of the two-step fallback not reported.
- **Topic**: routing
