# 277 — Choosing a deployment option (Together AI)
- **Source**: https://docs.together.ai/learn/choosing-a-deployment-option (Together AI, 2026-10)
- **Claim**: Together AI compares three ways to run a model (serverless per-token, dedicated endpoints per GPU-hour, dedicated containers with your own inference code) and gives a back-of-envelope crossover where dedicated GPUs become cheaper than per-token serverless.
- **Mechanism**: Serverless routes requests to a shared GPU pool billed per input/output token; no idle cost, no cold start, but TTFT variance and rate limits. Dedicated endpoints reserve GPUs billed per GPU-hour regardless of use; give predictable latency, custom or pinned models, and opt-in speculative decoding, quantization, prompt caching and batching; cold start takes minutes. Containers: own Docker image and inference stack on reserved GPUs. Crossover = daily GPU cost divided by serverless price per token. Hybrid pattern: dedicated endpoint for peak or latency-sensitive paths with overflow to serverless.
- **Numbers**: Example: dedicated H100 at around $3 per hour = $72 per day; serverless at $1 per 1M tokens; crossover about 72M tokens per day, or about 3M tokens per hour sustained. Serverless peak TTFT can be two to three times the average. Crossover said to stay roughly similar on H200 and B200 because per-hour rate and throughput both rise.
- **Token-cost implication**: Start on serverless; compute break-even (GPU $/day divided by $/token) against sustained utilization; move high-volume batch or latency-critical paths to dedicated; consider a mixed setup rather than all-in.
- **Caveats**: Vendor doc with an illustrative example (the $3/hour H100 differs from the $5.49/hour list price on the sibling Together pricing page, 278); crossover depends on model size, token mix, cache hit rate and GPU; no measurements.
- **Topic**: serving
