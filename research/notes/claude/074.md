# 074 — KV Cache Transform Coding for Compact Storage in LLM Inference
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01815 (NVIDIA / University of Warsaw, arXiv, 2025-11; ICLR 2026)
- **Claim**: kvtc compresses stored KV caches with a JPEG-style transform coder (PCA, adaptive quantization, entropy coding), reaching about 20x compression with accuracy within 1 score point of vanilla, and 40x or more for specific use cases. The goal is to keep reusable multi-turn caches on GPU or offloaded instead of recomputing them.
- **Mechanism**: One-time calibration per model: collect pre-RoPE keys/values (RoPE undone) from about 200K tokens (1:1 mix of 1-8K and 8-32K docs), concatenate across layers and heads, compute PCA via randomized SVD, and store the basis V and mean. A dynamic program assigns per-principal-component bit widths (groups of 1/16/64/256/1024 components with shared 16-bit shift and scale) under a bit budget; trailing components get zero bits. Compression: project, quantize, then DEFLATE via nvCOMP on GPU. Decoding always runs on the decompressed cache; compression is only for storage/transfer. The 128 most recent tokens and the 4 oldest (attention sink) tokens stay uncompressed. Decompression's inverse projection can run layer by layer so generation starts early.
- **Numbers**: 16-bit KV cache per 1K tokens: Llama 3.1 8B 128 MiB, Llama 3.3 70B 320 MiB, Mistral NeMo 12B 160 MiB, Qwen 2.5 R1 7B 56 MiB, R1 1.5B 28 MiB. Llama 3.1 8B at 18-22x total (kvtc16x before DEFLATE): GSM8K 56.9 vs 56.8 vanilla, MMLU 60.1 vs 60.5, Qasper 40.7 vs 40.4, LITM 99.3 vs 99.4, RULER-VT 99.1 vs 99.8. At 60-88x: GSM8K 57.2, MMLU 60.7, Qasper 37.8, LITM 90.2, RULER-VT 95.9. DEFLATE adds about 1.23x on average over quantization. At about 5-8x, KIVI/GEAR 2-bit lose accuracy on LITM (KIVI 88.8 vs 99.4) and H2O/TOVA collapse (LITM 20.2 and 1.2 on Llama 3.1 8B). Reasoning, 8-run average (R1 7B): AIME24 50.9 vanilla vs 52.5 kvtc8x (9-11x) vs 50.9 kvtc16x (18-21x); LiveCodeBench 36.7 vs 36.5 vs 31.6. R1 1.5B kvtc8x LiveCodeBench 16.1 vs 16.4 vanilla. Llama 3.3 70B on 4 GPUs MATH-500: 75.6 vanilla, 73.2 at 10x (-1.2pp row listed as kvtc10x 74.4), 72.6 at 20x (about 3.0pp drop). Latency on H100, Mistral NeMo 12B bf16: TTFT 3098 ms recompute vs 380 ms kvtc decompress at BS=8, 8K context; 1780 ms vs 208 ms at BS=2, 16K. Paper states TTFT reduction up to 8x vs recompute. Calibration: under 10 minutes on one H100 for a 12B model; stored basis about 2.4% of model parameters for Llama 3.3 70B.
- **Token-cost implication**: If you self-host and rely on prefix/KV reuse across turns (coding assistants, chat, agents), compressing cold KV caches stretches HBM/DRAM residency roughly in proportion to the ratio and avoids repeat prefill. Example in text: a 1,000-line file on Llama 3.3 70B is about 1.6 GiB of 8-bit KV. Cached-token discounts on hosted APIs are not affected; this only applies where you control the serving stack.
- **Caveats**: Authors' own results (NVIDIA), no independent replication in the text. Simulated multi-turn settings on benchmarks, not production traces. Dense decoder-only models 1.5B-70B only. Latency is from a simple Transformers-library implementation (not fused kernels). Reconstruction error used as a proxy for accuracy. Calibration stops at 200K tokens. Quality at 64x drops on LITM and Qasper. Some numbers above come from partly garbled table extraction (Table 4 row alignment); the 70B per-ratio values are least reliable.
