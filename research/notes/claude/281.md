# 281 — Batch inference (Gemini Enterprise)
- **Source**: https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/capabilities/batch-inference (Google, 2026-11)
- **Claim**: Gemini batch inference gives asynchronous, high-throughput processing at a 50% discount versus real-time inference, with a target of 24 hours turnaround.
- **Mechanism**: Submit one job (Cloud Storage or BigQuery input); the service validates, parallelizes and retries. Implicit caching is on by default for Gemini 2.5 and later. Jobs queue up to 72 hours; incomplete jobs after 24 hours of running are cancelled and only completed requests are billed. Cancelling returns completed work and bills only that. Not supported: Provisioned Throughput, explicit caching, RAG, tuned Gemini 3 and later models; image output limited to 1K. Batch is excluded from the SLA.
- **Numbers**: Batch 50% discount versus real-time. Implicit cache gives a 90% discount on cached input tokens; on Gemini 3 and later the cache and batch discounts stack to 95% total on cached input; on Gemini 2.5 they do not stack (90% takes precedence). Limits: 200,000 requests per job, 1GB Cloud Storage input file; avoid jobs under 1,000 requests. Embeddings: up to 1,000,000 rows per job, 10,000 concurrent queued jobs per project; legacy text embedding up to 30,000 prompts per request.
- **Token-cost implication**: Move non-urgent bulk work (classification, annotation, summarization, embeddings) to batch, combine small jobs into large ones, and keep shared prefixes for the stacked 95% cache discount on Gemini 3 and later.
- **Caveats**: Vendor doc; no latency guarantee beyond a 24-hour target and a 72-hour queue expiry; not an SLA-covered service.
- **Topic**: pricing
