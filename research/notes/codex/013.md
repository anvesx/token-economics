# 013 — Meta-Router: Bridging Gold-standard and Preference-based Evaluations in Large Language Model Routing
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25535 (arXiv, 2025-09)
- **Claim**: Routers trained on abundant preference judgments can be biased relative to scarce rubric- or expert-grounded quality labels. Treating the evaluation source as a causal treatment and estimating its query-dependent bias improves the quality–cost routing trade-off, especially when gold-standard data are limited.
- **Mechanism**: Embed queries, pool gold-standard and preference examples with a source indicator, and estimate the conditional shift `Δ(q) = gold quality gain − preference quality gain` using an R-learner or doubly robust learner. Add the estimated shift to preference labels to create pseudo-gold labels, fit a final regressor on true plus pseudo-gold data, and route to the premium model when its estimated gold-standard quality gain exceeds the cost-adjusted threshold.
- **Numbers**: HealthBench has 5,000 dialogues contributed by 262 physicians across 26 specialties and 60 countries; experiments used 768-dimensional Gemini embeddings reduced to 50 PCA dimensions, gold-standard sample sizes 100, 500, and 1,000, a 500-query test set, and 200 Monte Carlo rounds; PB–GS mean difference was about −0.47 with two-sided t-test `p < 2.2 × 10^-16`; PRBench has 1,100 tasks, 19,356 rubrics, 182 professionals, 114 countries, and 47 U.S. jurisdictions; experiments used 676 one-turn questions, 20 PCA dimensions, and gold-standard sample sizes 50, 100, and 150.
- **Token-cost implication**: Spend evaluation budget on a small, representative stream of trusted labels and use it to debias cheaper preference labels; tune the premium-model threshold from held-out gold labels, then monitor premium-route share, realized cost, and continuing gold quality.
- **Caveats**: Gold labels in the experiments were generated by GPT-5-mini with official rubrics rather than directly by experts; the authors note this limitation. The method assumes overlap/positivity between gold and preference query distributions, focuses on in-distribution routing and mainly two-model routing, uses normalized binary model cost in the main derivation, and reports efficiency curves rather than token-dollar savings.
- **Topic**: routing
