# 002 — RouterBench: A Benchmark for Multi-LLM Routing System
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12031 (arXiv, 2024-03)
- **Claim**: RouterBench standardizes cost–quality evaluation for multi-LLM routers and shows that comparable performance can differ in monetary cost by factors of 2–5×. Simple predictive routers can match the best individual models at similar or lower cost, but do not consistently beat a convex-hull baseline, while an oracle-quality cascade can.
- **Mechanism**: Precompute each model’s response, quality score, and inference cost for every prompt; represent models and router settings as points in a cost–quality plane; form a non-decreasing convex hull and summarize it with Average Improvement in Quality (AIQ). Predictive KNN/MLP routers estimate per-model performance from prompt embeddings and maximize `λ × predicted performance − cost`; cascading routers call models from cheap to expensive until a judge exceeds a threshold or the cost cap is reached.
- **Numbers**: 405,467 samples covering 11 models, 8 datasets, and 64 tasks; 800 RAG queries; predictive-router split 70% train/30% test; best KNN used 40 neighbors and `all-MiniLM-L12-v2`; best MLP used two 100-neuron hidden layers with learning rate 0.001; MMLU AIQ: Zero 0.763, KNN 0.773, MLP 0.769, zero-error cascade 0.901; RAG AIQ: Zero 0.632, cascade 0.657, MLP 0.675, KNN 0.695; cascade performance deteriorated rapidly when judge error exceeded 0.2; Oracle MMLU performance/cost 0.957/$0.297 versus GPT-4 0.828/$4.086; Oracle ARC performance/cost 0.977/$0.091 versus GPT-4 0.921/$2.286.
- **Token-cost implication**: Benchmark any router against the non-decreasing convex hull of the model pool, not only against the most expensive model; deploy routing only where it moves that frontier, and budget explicitly for judge errors in cascades.
- **Caveats**: The authors built and evaluated the benchmark; costs use contemporaneous provider/API prices, including Together AI as the open-model reference. The benchmark focuses on performance and dollar cost, omits latency and throughput, excludes many models/tasks, uses GPT-4 to grade MBPP, MT-Bench, and RAG, and the cascade’s strongest results assume an unrealistically accurate judge.
- **Topic**: routing
