# 069 — The Price Reversal Phenomenon: When Cheaper Reasoning Models End Up Costing More
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23971 (arXiv, 2026-03)
- **Claim**: Listed per-token price is an unreliable proxy for the actual bill from reasoning models because models consume different thinking tokens and agent turns. Across the study, lower-listed models frequently cost more in aggregate, and repeated runs of the same model and prompt also produced wide cost variation.
- **Mechanism**: Measure fresh input, output, thinking, cache-read/write tokens, and turns for each model on the same workload, then calculate actual spend from provider-specific prices. Attribute pairwise cost gaps across eight observable factors—turns, average fresh input/output/cache/thinking tokens per turn, and fresh-input/output/cache prices—using a Shapley-value decomposition; for deployment, predict and route on workload-specific cost distributions rather than static price ranks.
- **Numbers**: Study scope: 8 models, 12 datasets, 6,877 unique tasks, more than 7.39B generated tokens, over 190,000 API calls, and over $5,000 spend from February–May 2026. Of 336 comparisons (28 model pairs × 12 tasks), 106 or 32% reversed; task rates ranged from 11% on ArenaHard to 57% on MMLU-Pro, and reversal magnitude reached 28×. Gemini 3 Flash listed price was $3.50/1M tokens versus GPT-5.4 $17.50, yet aggregate cost was $705 versus $509 (38% higher); one MMLU-Pro query used more than 60,000 Gemini 3 Flash thinking tokens versus 25 for GPT-5.4, and one security task took 57 Kimi K2.6 turns versus 7 GPT-5.4 Mini turns. Same-query repeated-run cost spanned up to 9.7× with coefficient of variation routinely above 0.3. Mean reversal was 31.5%, Q15–Q40 was 39–40% with Q40 at 39.6%, and Q99 was 25.0%. May 1, 2026 prices per 1M input/output/cache tokens were GPT-5.4 $2.50/$15.00/$0.25, GPT-5.4 Mini $0.75/$4.50/$0.075, Gemini 3.1 Pro $2.00/$12.00/$0.20, Gemini 3 Flash $0.50/$3.00/$0.05, Opus 4.7 $5.00/$25.00/$0.50, Haiku 4.5 $1.00/$5.00/$0.10, Kimi K2.6 $0.95/$4.00/$0.16, and MiniMax M2.7 $0.30/$1.20/$0.06.
- **Token-cost implication**: Benchmark total cost per successful task on production-shaped queries before routing, log thinking tokens, cache tokens, and turn counts per request, and budget from cost quantiles rather than multiplying expected tokens by list price. Cap or intervene on excessive reasoning and multi-turn loops because replayed history makes agent cost grow super-linearly with turns.
- **Caveats**: This is an author-run study using one May 1, 2026 price snapshot, one reasoning setting per model, provider-recommended decoding, and specific agent scaffolds; prices and rankings can change. It deliberately analyzes cost separately from answer quality, some free-form tasks use an LLM grader, and its exact reversal rates may not transfer to another workload; the paper says data and code will be released.
- **Topic**: pricing
