# 015 — RouterDC: Query-Based Router by Dual Contrastive Learning for Assembling Large Language Models
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19886 (NeurIPS, 2024-09)
- **Claim**: A router trained to recognize multiple suitable models per query outperforms top-1 and score-distribution routing baselines on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution tasks. It achieves the gain with one selected-model call at inference rather than querying the whole ensemble.
- **Mechanism**: Score every candidate model on labeled training queries; encode each query with mDeBERTaV3-base and learn one embedding per LLM. A sample–LLM contrastive loss pulls the query toward the top three model embeddings and away from the bottom three with score below 0.5; a sample–sample contrastive loss clusters related queries, pulls same-cluster embeddings together, and pushes different-cluster embeddings apart; select the nearest model embedding at inference.
- **Numbers**: 7 candidate LLMs; router encoder 86M parameters and total under 100M; 768-dimensional model embeddings; open-ended training scores use 10 beam-sampled outputs at temperature 0.2; training used 1,000 steps, learning rate `5 × 10^-5`, weight decay 0.01, batch size 64, and one NVIDIA A100 80GB; in-distribution average accuracy 58.54% versus best individual 54.56%, ZOOTER 53.97%, and Voting 54.37%; OOD average 45.85% versus best individual 43.95% and ZOOTER 40.16%; training took 28.3 minutes; inference was about 6× faster than Voting; with 30 samples/task RouterDC reached 57.21% versus prior SOTA 55.77%.
- **Token-cost implication**: Use a one-call router when several models are interchangeable for a query; preserve multiple positives during training, then add explicit cost to the model score when optimizing the operating point.
- **Caveats**: The authors evaluated mostly 7B/8B open-source models and accuracy-based tasks; output sampling makes offline label generation expensive. The main experiments optimize accuracy rather than API token cost, the cost-aware result is limited to GSM8K and MBPP, chat-history routing is untested, and larger frontier models are left for future work.
- **Topic**: routing
