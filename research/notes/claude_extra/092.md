# 092 — Efficient Reasoning for Large Reasoning Language Models via Certainty-Guided Reflection Suppression
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05337 (Peking University / HKUST-Guangzhou / Huawei, arXiv, 2025-08; AAAI 2026 copyright notice)
- **Claim**: Reasoning models keep generating "Wait"/"Alternatively" reflection after already reaching a confident answer. Suppressing those trigger tokens at decode time, in proportion to measured certainty, cuts output tokens with little accuracy loss and no retraining.
- **Mechanism**: Training-free decoding change (CGRS). At each "\n\n" checkpoint, a side probe appends "**Final Answer: \boxed" and generates a tentative answer a. Certainty C = 1 - (mean token entropy of a) / log|V|, in [0,1]. Suppression probability p = max(0, (C - delta)/(1 - delta)), delta = 0.9. With probability p, logits of reflection-trigger tokens (Wait, But, Alternatively, Hmm and their vocabulary variants, chosen by frequency analysis on R1-Distill-Qwen-7B traces on AIME24 and AMC23) are set to a large negative value before sampling. The probe runs separately from main decoding. Implemented in vLLM.
- **Numbers**: Average token reduction 18.5% to 41.9% across models (Qwen3-4B/8B/14B/32B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B and Llama-8B, QwQ-32B) over AIME24, AMC23, MATH500, GPQA-Diamond, with accuracy drop up to 3%. Qwen3-14B: 25.4% length reduction with average accuracy 80.1 to 80.8; NoThinking got 77.3% reduction but 17.8% accuracy drop, DEER 38.8% reduction with 5.7% drop. QwQ-32B: CGRS 30.5% reduction; the other baselines gave only minor reductions. NoThinking on MATH500 with R1-Distill-Qwen-7B: 65.4% shorter, 9.4% accuracy drop. Table 2 (AMC23, R1-Distill-Qwen-7B): vanilla 87.5 accuracy, 5861 tokens; fixed p=0.25: 81.7, 3729; p=0.5: 80.0, 3266; p=1.0: 76.7, 2373; certainty-guided: 88.3, 3406 (41.9% shorter). Delta ablation (same setting): delta 0.9: 88.3% accuracy, LR 41.9; 0.5: 76.2%, 48.5; 0.1: 72.5%, 53.7. Settings: temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, 3 runs averaged.
- **Token-cost implication**: If you self-host open reasoning models, a logit mask on reflection tokens gated by a cheap answer-probe entropy can cut reasoning tokens by about a fifth to two fifths. Keep delta high (0.9); lowering it trades accuracy for length. It costs extra probe forward passes at every "\n\n", so measure net throughput. Not usable on hosted APIs that hide logits and thinking.
- **Caveats**: Authors' own experiments on open models 4B to 32B, one decoding setting, small benchmarks (AIME24 30 problems, AMC23 40, GPQA-D 198). Does not report latency or probe compute overhead. Ablations run on a single model and dataset. The text of the delta ablation labels the column LR with a "lower is better" arrow although higher LR means more compression.
