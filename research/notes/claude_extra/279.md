# 279 — Serverless models (Together AI)
- **Source**: https://docs.together.ai/docs/serverless-models (Together AI, date not given in file)
- **Claim**: Together's catalog page lists its serverless models with per-1M-token input, cached-input and output prices, context length and quantization, plus per-image, per-video and audio prices. It states US-only inference costs 25% more.
- **Mechanism**: Pay-per-token with no provisioning or minimum. Chat models carry separate input, cached input and output rates, and many are served quantized (FP8, NVFP4, FP4, MXFP4). Geo-specific inference through us-api.together.ai is billed at 1.25x the standard rates. Image models bill per image or per megapixel with `Cost = MP x Price per MP x (Steps / Default Steps)`; extra steps raise cost, fewer steps do not lower it.

- **Token-cost implication**: Open-weight models on Together span about 100x in output price ($0.25 to $15.00 per 1M), so route simple work to the Flash or small tiers. Cached-input discounts vary by model, so prompt-prefix reuse is worth most on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Budget 25% extra if data must stay in the US. Choose image resolution deliberately because cost scales with megapixels.
- **Caveats**: Vendor price list as captured in this text; not a benchmark and no quality comparison. Some rows show "-" for cached price (unknown whether unsupported). Image and video prices are described as estimates passing through provider charges. Prices can change.
