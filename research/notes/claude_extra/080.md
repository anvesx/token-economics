# 080 — LongLLMLingua: Accelerating and Enhancing LLMs in Long Context Scenarios via Prompt Compression
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06839 (Microsoft Research, 2023-10; v2 Aug 2024)
- **Claim**: A question-aware prompt compression method that uses a small LM to drop tokens from long prompts, cutting cost and latency while improving accuracy and reducing "lost in the middle" position bias for black-box LLMs.
- **Mechanism**: Four parts on top of LLMLingua (LLaMA-2-7B-Chat as the small model). (1) Question-aware coarse compression: rank each document by the perplexity of the question (plus the restrictive sentence "We can get the answer to this question in the given documents") conditioned on that document; keep top documents. (2) Question-aware fine compression: score tokens by contrastive perplexity, perplexity(token | history) minus perplexity(token | question, history), equivalent to conditional pointwise mutual information; iterative token dropping in 200-token segments. (3) Document reordering by relevance score so key content sits at the start. (4) Dynamic compression ratio: more relevant documents get a higher keep ratio (linear scheduler, delta tau 0.3; instruction ratio 0.85, question 0.9, coarse coefficient k=2). (5) Subsequence recovery: after the LLM answers, map substrings of the answer that occur in the compressed prompt back to the original text (fixes mangled entities).
- **Numbers**: Target LLMs GPT-3.5-Turbo-0613 (16k variant for over 4k tokens) and LongChat-13B-16k. NaturalQuestions (20 docs, avg 2,946 tokens): up to +21.4% accuracy with about 4x fewer tokens (ground-truth doc at 10th position); at 4x constraint (about 748 tokens) 75.0/71.8/71.2/71.2/74.7 for positions 1/5/10/15/20 vs original 75.7/57.3/54.1/55.4/63.1. LongBench (avg 10,289 tokens) at 2,000-token constraint: avg 48.3 at 1,822 tokens (6x) vs original 44.0 at 10,295 tokens; at 3,000 tokens avg 48.8. ZeroSCROLLS 2,000 constraint avg 32.7 vs original 32.5 (9,788 tokens). MuSiQue 2x: F1 51.2 vs 45.8 original. LooGLE (avg 30,546 tokens) at about 10x: avg 32.1 vs 22.6 original. Latency (V100-32G, includes compression time): 1.4x-2.6x end-to-end speedup for roughly 10k-token prompts at 2x-6x compression; 1.4x at 2x, 2.0x at 4x on NQ. Cost per 1,000 samples on GPT-3.5-Turbo pricing: Multi-doc QA $4.6 to $1.3 (-71.7%), LongBench $31.5 to $3.0 (-90.5%), ZeroSCROLLS $30.6 to $3.2 (-89.5%), MuSiQue $3.8 to $1.8 (-52.6%), LooGLE $93.6 to $5.6 (-94.0%). Ablation: removing question-awareness in coarse step drops NQ 2x scores from about 77.2 (1st) to 42.1.
- **Token-cost implication**: For RAG and long-document QA on a pay-per-token API, compress retrieved context before sending (question-aware, with a relevance-reordered doc order) rather than sending all documents; expect 2x-6x fewer input tokens and often equal or better accuracy. Budget for the compressor's own compute and for the fact that it cannot be cached across questions.
- **Caveats**: Authors' own results (Microsoft), mostly GPT-3.5-Turbo (an old model) and LongChat-13B; costs are estimated from list pricing, not billed. The paper states it must re-compress per question (no context caching) and roughly doubles LLMLingua's compute. Latency measured on a V100 with the small model locally. Accuracy gains partly reflect fixing lost-in-the-middle on weak long-context models. Complex, subtle context-question relations might hurt coarse-level selection.
- **Topic**: compression
