# 161 — Billing
- **Source**: https://kiro.dev/docs/billing (Amazon Kiro, 2026-08; page updated November 1, 2026)
- **Claim**: Kiro bills by credit-based subscription tiers, with credits consumed fractionally per request, and from November 1, 2026 adds a first-upgrade free trial and per-second proration.
- **Mechanism**: Five tiers (Free, Pro, Pro+, Pro Max, Power) each with a monthly credit allotment; add-on credits can be bought beyond plan limits (not on Free). Credits are consumed fractionally by request: simple edits and shorter prompts use fewer than complex, lengthy tasks. Plan credits reset each billing cycle; trial credits do not reset. Proration: prorated fee and credits = (seconds remaining / total seconds in month) x monthly fee or credits. Downgrades take effect the following month (full current-month fee charged); upgrades are immediate. After upgrade, the new plan's credits are used before add-on credits.
- **Numbers**: Credits per tier: Free 50, Pro 1,000, Pro+ 2,000, Pro Max 5,000, Power 10,000. Pro is $20/month with 1,000 credits; Pro+ is $40 (stated in the example). Worked example (30-day month, upgrade at 00:00 UTC day 15, 1,382,400 of 2,592,000 seconds left): Pro fee $10.67, 533 credits; Pro+ fee $21.33, 1,067 credits; Pro Max fee $53.33, 2,667 credits; Power fee $106.67, 5,333 credits. First upgrade from Free (from Nov 1, 2026): rest of month on Pro free, plus 500 trial credits that expire 12 months after receipt. Implied (derived, not stated): Pro $20 per 1,000 credits.
- **Token-cost implication**: In credit-metered agent products, cost scales with task size, so shorter prompts and simple edits cost less per request; budget in credits per task rather than per token. Pooled or purchased add-on credits are the overflow path, and plan choice should follow credit burn rate.
- **Caveats**: Vendor billing doc; does not state how many tokens a credit equals or per-model credit rates (refers to the pricing page). Only some models are on the Free tier. Prices for Pro Max and Power are only derivable from the example table.
