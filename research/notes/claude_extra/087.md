# 087 — Provence: Efficient and Robust Context Pruning for Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16214 (NAVER LABS Europe, arXiv, 2025-01)
- **Claim**: A small DeBERTa-v3 model that prunes retrieved passages sentence by sentence, picks the amount of pruning per passage, and can be merged with the reranker so pruning costs almost nothing. It cuts about 50-80% of context with little or no QA quality loss.
- **Mechanism**: Context pruning is framed as per-token binary sequence labeling over the concatenated question and passage. Training labels ("silver") come from Llama-3-8B-Instruct, prompted to answer using only the context and cite sentence numbers as [i], or output "No answer"; cited sentences become positive labels, samples with no citation and no "No answer" are dropped. Training data: MS MARCO document ranking train set (370k queries, passages of 1-10 sentences, top-5 per query from SPLADE-v3 plus DeBERTa-v3 reranker); NQ (87k queries) used in ablations. Unification: a reranking head on the BOS token and a pruning head on passage tokens in one cross-encoder; the pruning loss is added to an MSE distillation loss to the original reranker score (lambda 0.05, learning rate 3e-6, batch 48, one epoch). At inference, a threshold T binarizes token probabilities; "sentence rounding" keeps a sentence if more than 0.5 of its tokens are kept. T=0.1 prunes less, T=0.5 more; the same T worked across domains. Pipeline: retrieve, rerank+prune in one forward pass, generate.
- **Numbers**: Setup: top-5 passages, Llama-2-7B-chat generator, LLM-judge score. Compression ranges 50%-80% depending on dataset at T=0.1/0.5. NQ: full context 71.8 LLMEval vs Provence (standalone) 72.3 at 64.1% compression and 70.6 at 77.3%. HotpotQA: full 57.0 vs 56.6 at 69.5% and 54.8 at 84.1%. PopQA: full 57.8 vs 59.0 at 69.9% (improvement from noise removal). SyllabusQA: full 52.9 vs 50.7 at 64.1% and 47.8 at 76.6%. RGB: full 93.5 vs 95.8 at 61.6%. Cost at 50 samples, batch 1: LongLLMLingua 194 s (5.4e16 FLOPs), LLMLingua2 38 s, RECOMP extractive 12 s, RECOMP abstractive 499 s, DSLR 26 s, Provence standalone 25 s (7.9e14 FLOPs); unified model adds almost nothing. Generation speedup at 49% compression, V100 32GB with vLLM: Llama-2-7B 1.2x (batch 1) and 2x (batch 256); Llama-2-13B 1.4x and 2x; SOLAR-10.7B 1.4x and 1.9x. Reranking preserved: NQ R@5 84.5 vs 83.0 for DeBERTa-v3 baseline; BEIR avg nDCG@10 55.9 vs 55.4. LLMLingua2 at 70% compression on NQ drops to 59.5 (rate 0.25 setting).
- **Token-cost implication**: In a RAG pipeline that already has a cross-encoder reranker, swap in a pruning-capable reranker to cut context tokens roughly in half before the generator. That shrinks billed input tokens and prefill time at essentially no added latency. Use a threshold rather than a fixed compression ratio, and test on your own domain.
- **Caveats**: Authors' own evaluation (NAVER LABS). QA only, one passage processed at a time, English only. Main generator is Llama-2-7B-chat; the paper has no dollar-cost figures for hosted APIs, so savings are token and FLOP based. Speedups measured on a V100, smaller at batch 1. Silver labels depend on Llama-3-8B-Instruct quality. Multi-passage and non-QA tasks were not tested.
