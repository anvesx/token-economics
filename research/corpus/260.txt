FAQs

Your inference questions, answered.

What teams ask before they move production traffic to Fireworks.

Is Fireworks OpenAI and Anthropic compatible?

Yes. Fireworks supports OpenAI’s Chat Completions and Responses APIs and Anthropic’s Messages API. Change the base URL and API key, choose a model, and keep the rest of your code.
How fast do new models become available?

As an official launch partner for all major model providers, we often provide day zero support for major launches.
Which model should I use?

Start with our recommendations by use case. It covers coding and agentic tasks, agents with tool use, general reasoning and planning, long context and summarization, fast extraction and classification, vision and document understanding, audio and video, and embeddings and reranking. For coding, consider our intelligent routing model.
What models and modalities are supported?

As an official launch partner for all major model providers, we often provide day zero support for major launches. Qwen, Kimi, DeepSeek, MiniMax, Nemotron, GLM, Muse, and many more across text and vision language models. The full list of hundreds of models is in the model library.
How do you protect my data?

Zero data retention is the default. We don’t store your prompts or outputs, and we never train on them. Fireworks is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001 certified, supports HIPAA with a BAA, and offers account-level data residency. Audit reports are in the Trust Center.
Can I run this in my own cloud?

Yes. With Bring-Your-Own-Cloud (BYOC), the Fireworks serving stack runs on your Kubernetes cluster, in your cloud or data center. We deploy it, tune it and run it, and inference stays inside your network. You call it through the same API you use for Serverless.
What is covered by the SLA

Reserved Throughput comes with a throughput SLA for all traffic within your reservation. Serverless doesn’t carry an SLA. If you need uptime commitments for dedicated or BYOC deployments, talk to our team. Live status is published at status.fireworks.ai.
