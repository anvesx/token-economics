B.3. Confidence Width Calibration
We evaluate the tightness of the theoretical confidence width
for the predicted satisfaction probabilities. At T = 1000,
the raw theoretical width is highly conservative in our eval-
uated data. We also evaluate a calibrated width obtained
by applying a fixed scaling factor of 0.005. The calibrated
width covers 83.4% of the observed errors in this experi-
ment, which justifies our choice of multiplying the theoreti-
cal confidence radius αt by 0.005.

across independent trials. For each independent trial, we
run the algorithm for T = 1000 rounds and record whether
the realized average satisfaction meets the target 1−ϵ = 0.8.
We repeat this procedure over 5000 independent trials and
report the empirical constraint-satisfaction frequency with
95% confidence intervals. Figure 8 compares this empirical
